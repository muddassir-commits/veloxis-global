## 4. Business needs underneath (what they really want)

- **Proof of legitimacy fast**: RERA registration number + QR code visible above the fold (now a legal requirement in Maharashtra, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu and expanding), so buyers/brokers don't dismiss the page as a "fly-by-night builder."
- **Speed on cheap Android + patchy 4G**: most Indian property searches happen on mobile; pages that take 6–8 seconds lose buyers before they see anything — target is sub-3-second load.
- **Low-friction lead capture**: fewer form fields, WhatsApp click-to-chat as an alternative to a form (buyers are wary of "15 calls from aggressive salespeople" after filling a form).
- **Pricing transparency**: price sheet / starting price shown instead of "Price on Request" — buyers bounce off vague pricing.
- **Brochure/e-brochure gating**: capture phone/WhatsApp before releasing the PDF brochure or floor plan pack.
- **Site-visit booking as the real conversion goal**, not just form fills — because 70–90%+ of leads never show up without a strong follow-up/booking mechanism.
- **CRM / WhatsApp / call integration** wired into the page (not just a static "contact us" form) so no lead sits untouched.
- **Channel partner (CP) enablement**: a way for brokers/CPs to get a trackable, brandable link/portal to the same microsite so their leads are attributed correctly and commissions aren't disputed.
- **Separate, focused page per project/tower/phase** rather than one generic company website — buyers searching "[project name] + [locality]" expect a dedicated page, not a buried listing.
- **Trust and social proof**: RERA badge, possession-status/construction-progress updates, testimonials, media mentions, "as seen in," amenity renders, location map with connectivity call-outs.
- **Cost efficiency / ROI accountability**: agencies and builders want to see cost-per-lead and cost-per-site-visit, not vanity traffic numbers.
- **Own the audience, not just rent it**: builders increasingly want a project website they control (SEO equity, retargeting pixels, owned data) rather than depending solely on 99acres/MagicBricks/Housing listings whose lead quality and data ownership they can't verify.
- **Fast turnaround for launch windows**: pre-launch/soft-launch timelines are short and marketing-compliance-sensitive post-RERA, so builders want a page live before the ad campaign starts, not weeks later.
- **Multi-channel ad-readiness**: page must be "plug & play" for Meta/Google/YouTube campaigns with UTM tracking, pixel/CAPI setup already wired in.

## 5. Industry vocabulary

- EOI (Expression of Interest) — non-binding early-buyer intent form/token, often used before formal booking
- Pre-launch / soft-launch — marketing stages before/after formal RERA-compliant launch (pre-launch selling before RERA registration is now effectively illegal, so language must be careful)
- Possession — date/status when the unit is physically handed over
- Inventory — unsold unit stock across a project (by tower/floor/type)
- Microsite — dedicated single-project web presence separate from the main corporate site
- Floor plan / unit plan — layout diagrams per configuration (1/2/3/4 BHK)
- Price sheet / cost sheet — itemized pricing per unit/floor/configuration
- Brochure / e-brochure — downloadable PDF marketing collateral, often gated behind a lead form
- BHK — bedroom-hall-kitchen configuration shorthand (e.g., 2BHK, 3BHK)
- CP (Channel Partner) — RERA-registered broker/agency reselling on behalf of the developer
- CP portal / CP login — broker-facing dashboard to register leads and track commission
- Site visit — physical/guided tour of the project or sample flat
- Sample flat / show flat — furnished model unit for site visits
- RERA number / RERA registration — mandatory project registration number that must appear in all ads including websites
- QR code disclosure — RERA-mandated scannable code linking to project's official registration record, now required on digital ads/websites in several states
- Booking amount / token amount — initial payment to reserve a unit
- Super built-up area / carpet area / RERA carpet area — differing area-measurement terms buyers compare
- Township / integrated township — large multi-phase mixed-use development
- Possession-linked payment plan / CLP (Construction Linked Plan) / down payment plan (DPP) — payment structures shown on landing pages
- Cost per lead (CPL) / cost per site visit (CPSV) — core paid-media KPIs agencies quote
- Lead-to-site-visit ratio / site-visit-to-booking ratio — funnel health metrics
- Landing page vs microsite vs corporate website — distinct deliverables clients often confuse
- 3D walkthrough / virtual tour / 360° view — immersive project visualization assets
- Amenities — clubhouse, gym, pool etc. list, standard section on every microsite
- Locality/connectivity map — proximity call-outs (metro, highway, schools) used for local SEO and buyer decision-making
