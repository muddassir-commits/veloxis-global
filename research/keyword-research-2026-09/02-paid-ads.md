## 4. Business needs underneath

- **Lead quality over lead volume** — buyers explicitly distrust low CPL as a vanity metric; they want cost-per-site-visit and cost-per-booking reporting, not just cost-per-form.
- **Proof the leads are exclusive**, not shared/recycled the way portal (99acres/MagicBricks/Housing.com) leads are — this is a direct differentiator agencies use against portals.
- **Speed-to-lead infrastructure** — WhatsApp auto-response within seconds/60 seconds, CRM auto-routing, because response-time is repeatedly tied to pickup-rate and conversion (10-minute rule, follow-up cadence data).
- **Fake/junk lead filtering** — form friction, qualifying questions (budget/locality), interest-detail targeting, excluding renters/job-seekers/students, offline conversion upload to stop Smart Bidding from optimizing toward junk.
- **Compliance cover** — RERA number/QR code on every creative including social, correct Meta Special Ad Category setup to avoid ad rejection/account bans, avoidance of misleading claims (RERA/ASCI scrutiny).
- **Call/lead tracking and attribution** — unique tracked numbers per campaign, CRM tagging by source (Meta/Google/portal/walk-in/CP), because multi-channel buyer journeys make manual attribution unreliable.
- **Follow-up systems, not just ad delivery** — clients want the agency (or its recommended CRM/telecalling layer) to fix the 2-call-then-give-up problem, since conversions often land on the 5th–8th touch.
- **Budget efficiency guidance** — clear minimums to "exit the learning phase" (Meta) or get enough conversion data (Google), tiered budget-to-lead-volume expectations by property segment (affordable/mid/luxury).
- **Combined-channel strategy** — Google for high-intent/bottom-funnel searchers, Meta for awareness/volume/retargeting; clients want an agency that runs both in a funnel rather than picking one.
- **Self-service digital presence for brokers/CPs** — many have no website or Google Business Profile; they need landing pages + ads + lead capture bundled since they lack the time/skill to build it themselves (this is exactly Veloxis's 3-part offering).
- **Brand/competitor keyword defense** — protecting project name searches from competitor bidding, running cheap defensive brand campaigns.
- **Localized, vernacular creative** — regional-language ads and real site photography/video outperform generic renders/stock creative on both CTR and CPL.
