3. What we fixed

Structure

  • /case-studies → /playbooks. Four example plans with real research, keyword groups, bidding stages, landing page blueprint, WhatsApp flow and CP lead registration — each labelled “example plan, not a client result”.
  • /testimonials removed (placeholders), redirected to /playbooks.
  • New /channel-partners page; /industries/real-estate repositioned for developers and builders. Both built from one template.
  • 3 new guides targeting 880 searches/month each: EOI in real estate, channel partner in real estate, real estate ad examples.

Content

  • Service pages rewritten around searched terms, with problems, deliverables, process, who it’s for, 7–8 FAQs and links to related services, playbooks and guides.
  • Homepage: keyword-led H1, honest trust line, “Who we help”, playbooks, service-area section and 7 FAQs.
  • About, Contact, Pricing, footer and CTA banner rewritten without superlatives or unverifiable claims.
  • 4 existing blog posts rewritten to 625–780 words each with sources where facts are cited; the Google vs Meta post keeps its real May 2026 date.

Technical

  • One site-wide @graph: Organization + ProfessionalService (service-area business, no address, no rating), WebSite, founder Person.
  • Per page: Service, BlogPosting, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, AboutPage, ContactPage, CollectionPage — each once.
  • Central metadata registry; titles render exactly as written; Open Graph and Twitter cards on every page; article dates on posts.
  • Sitemap, llms.txt and llms-full.txt generated from the data files, so they can’t go stale.
  • GA4 generate_lead, booking_click and cta_click events; phone and WhatsApp clicks now include the source page.
