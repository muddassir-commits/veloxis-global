## Don't target (checked)
- **real estate crm** (2,400): the top 10 is CRM software lists.
- **builder website** (60,500): means website-builder tools, not builders.
- **channel partner meaning** (1,300): generic business definition.
- **how to sell property / flat fast** (210–260): homeowners, not builders.
- **leads example** (720): generic.
- **City terms**: "real estate digital marketing agency in delhi" gets 40. Noida, Lucknow, Gurgaon and Kanpur variants get **0**. Still no case for city pages.
- **Pain phrases** ("junk leads", "fake leads", "site visits"): 0 volume. Use them in copy and FAQs, not as targets.

## Copy rules from this research
- Write "Meta (Facebook) ads" wherever it fits. People in India now search "meta ads" more than "facebook ads".
- Lead with lead quality, not lead count. That's what buyers complain about.
- "AI" is the fastest-rising word in this space. Pair it with WhatsApp and chatbot on the automation page.
