Executing the 3-Stage Project Sales Lifecycle

Stage 1: Pre-Launch Foundation (Day -90 to Day 0)

The pre-launch phase focuses on positioning, asset creation, and channel partner onboarding. During this window, developers build high-speed Next.js project microsites, configure CRM pipelines, produce high-resolution 3D renders, and host closed-door conclaves for top local channel partners. No public ads are released until the official RERA certificate is received.

Stage 2: The Grand Launch Blitz (Month 1 to Month 3)

Once RERA registration is active, the omnichannel blitz is deployed simultaneously across outdoor unipoles, newspaper announcements, and aggressive digital ad scaling. The objective is maximum local dominance and collecting initial Expressions of Interest (EOIs) to sell out the inaugural tower at introductory pricing.

Stage 3: Sustained Velocity & Construction Milestones (Month 4 to Month 18)

After launch excitement wanes, the marketing plan shifts to milestone-driven storytelling. Ads feature actual drone construction progress, slab completion celebrations, and customer testimonial interviews. Retargeting funnels keep the project top-of-mind for undecided buyers who enquired earlier.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to Enforce Weekly

A marketing plan is only as good as its weekly governance. Developers must review four operational KPIs every Monday morning:

  • Cost Per Qualified Lead (CPQL): Not raw inquiries, but leads verified for budget match and phone validity.
  • Cost Per Site Visit (CPSV): Total marketing spend divided by verified physical site tours (benchmark: ₹3,500 – ₹7,000 in North India).
  • Lead-to-Visit Ratio: Percentage of digital leads completing a site tour (target: ≥ 10%).
  • Visit-to-Booking Ratio: Closing effectiveness of the on-site sales team (target: ≥ 8% to 12%).
