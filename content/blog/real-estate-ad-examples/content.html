Good real estate ads answer a buyer's four questions (where is it, how much, how far, can I trust the builder) and end with one clear action, such as "Get the price sheet on WhatsApp". Below are 24 original real estate ad examples for India, sorted by ad type (Meta lead ads, Instagram reels, Google search ads, hoardings, newspaper, WhatsApp and portal listings), each with sample copy, why it works and where the RERA number goes.

The examples are written for developers, channel partners and real estate agents. They are templates to adapt, not real campaigns, and we don't attach performance numbers to them, because results depend on your project, price and follow-up. Replace everything in [brackets] and every ₹X with your real, RERA-consistent details.

What must every real estate ad in India include?

Every ad for a registered project must prominently show the project's RERA registration number and the state RERA authority's website, under Section 11(2) of the RERA Act, 2016; a project that must be registered can't be advertised before registration (Section 3). Some states add a QR code: MahaRERA made it mandatory on ads from 1 August 2023 (Housing.com), and UP RERA asks for it on brochures and ads (PTI, July 2024). Agents should add their own RERA agent number.

This is where many ads go wrong. In its Half-Yearly Complaints Report 2024-25, ASCI named real estate the most violative sector (34% of cases investigated from April to September 2024), and 99% of the 1,027 real estate ads it processed for MahaRERA were found in violation. Facebook and Meta lead ad examples (1–4)

  1. The price-band filter. Copy: "2 & 3 BHK in [locality] from ₹X lakh*. Possession [month year] as per RERA. Get the price sheet on WhatsApp." Why it works: the price band filters out people outside your budget before they fill the form. RERA: number and website on the image and in the text.
  2. The configuration chooser. Image: three floor plans side by side. Copy: "Which one fits your family? 2 BHK [X] sq ft, 3 BHK [Y] sq ft carpet area. Tap to see prices." The form's first question repeats the choice. Why it works: the buyer self-selects, which gives your sales team a qualified first question. RERA: bottom strip of the image.
  3. The commute answer. Copy: "[X] minutes from [metro station], timed on a weekday morning. Watch the drive." Why it works: it answers "how far is it really?" with something the buyer can check. Keep the time honest; filmed proof beats a claim. RERA: end card and text.
  4. The site visit weekend. Copy: "Site visits this Saturday and Sunday, 10 am to 6 pm. Free pickup from [landmark]. Choose a slot." Why it works: it asks for a small, specific step rather than a purchase. Best for retargeting people who already watched your videos. RERA: on the creative.

Instagram reel ad hooks for property (5–8)

  1. "Don't buy in [locality] until you see this balcony." Opens on the view from the sample flat. Why it works: a curiosity hook that pays off in the first seconds with something real.
  2. "What ₹X lakh gets you in [locality]." A straight walkthrough with the carpet area on screen. Why it works: it matches what buyers compare across projects. Use the price only if it is the real starting price.
  3. "Month [N] on site." Construction progress with the date on screen, posted monthly. Why it works: for under-construction projects, regular proof of progress builds trust that a render can't.
  4. "3 things to check before you book a flat." A short checklist (RERA number, carpet area, approved banks) filmed at your site. Why it works: it helps the buyer first, and it is an easy way for a real estate agent to look credible. RERA: on screen at the end and in the caption.

Google search ad examples for real estate (9–12)

Google responsive search ads allow up to 15 headlines of 30 characters and 4 descriptions of 90 characters (Google Ads Help), so every word must match the search:

  1. Project-name ad: Headlines "[Project] Official Site" | "2 & 3 BHK from ₹X Lakh*" | "RERA Registered". Why it works: it protects your name from competitors and portals bidding on it.
  2. Locality ad: "3 BHK in [Locality]" | "Near [Metro Station]" | "Possession [Year]". Why it works: it mirrors exactly how buyers search.
  3. Ready-to-move ad: "Ready to Move 2 BHK" | "OC Received" | "Visit This Weekend". Why it works: ready inventory with an occupancy certificate answers the buyer's biggest worry. Only say "OC received" if it is true.
  4. Plot ad: "Plots in [Locality]" | "[Authority]-Approved Layout" | "Get the Site Map". Why it works: plot buyers want approvals and the layout first (see how to sell plots online).

Send each to a project landing page that repeats the ad's promise, not the homepage. The full keyword setup is in Google Ads for real estate.

Hoarding and outdoor ad examples (13–15)

  1. The distance board. "[Project]: [X] km ahead, turn left at [landmark]." Why it works: on a road, people read a few words at most; one fact and one direction is enough.
  2. The question board. "Still paying rent in [area]?" with the project name and a QR code. Why it works: it speaks to one clear buyer, the renter nearby. QR and RERA number large and high.
  3. The site board. On the project boundary: "Sample flat open daily. Walk in." with the RERA number. Why it works: it turns passing traffic into walk-ins.

Newspaper ad examples (16–18)

  1. The launch announcement. Headline: "Bookings open [date] at [project], [locality]." One real photo, the price band, a QR code to a tagged landing page. Why it works: print is strong for a single big date.
  2. The price-release ad. "Launch price valid till [date]", with the terms printed in readable type. Why it works: a dated, specific offer gives a reason to act now. Keep it true and say what the conditions are.
  3. The property-supplement listing. Quarter page in the weekend property section: project, configuration, carpet area, a map with three landmarks and a dedicated phone number. Why it works: readers of that section are already looking.

WhatsApp message ad examples (19–21)

Send these only to people who enquired or opted in, never as bulk cold messages, and follow WhatsApp's business policy.

  1. The instant reply. "Hi [name], thanks for asking about [project]. Here is the price sheet and floor plan. Which configuration are you looking at?" Why it works: it delivers what was promised and asks one question.
  2. The click-to-WhatsApp ad opener. Ad copy: "Message us for today's price list." Pre-filled chat: "Hi, please send the [project] price list." Why it works: starting a chat feels lighter than filling a form (see WhatsApp's ads page).
  3. The visit reminder. "Your site visit is tomorrow at 11 am. Here is the location pin. Reply 1 to confirm, 2 to change." Why it works: it reduces no-shows with one easy action. More templates are in our WhatsApp message templates.

Property portal listing examples (22–24)

  1. The specific title. "3 BHK, [X] sq ft carpet, east-facing, [locality], near [school/metro]" instead of "Luxurious flat for sale". Why it works: portal search results are scanned; specifics win.
  2. The honest photo set. Real daylight photos of every room, the view and the building entrance, in the order a visitor would walk. Why it works: buyers distrust listings with only renders or stock photos.
  3. The description that answers questions. Price, maintenance, parking, floor, possession or OC status, RERA number and how to book a visit. Why it works: it saves a phone call, and answers come from the listing rather than a guess. See 99acres vs MagicBricks for choosing portals, including their free and paid listing options.

Copy formulas that work for property ads

  • Where + what + how much: "3 BHK near [metro] from ₹X Cr*".
  • Problem + answer: "Long commute? [X] minutes to the expressway." Use a time you can show.
  • Proof: "Tower B: [N] floors complete. See this month's site video."
  • One clear action: "Get the price sheet on WhatsApp" is clearer than "Enquire now".

How to create a real estate ad: step by step

  1. Pick one buyer (first-time buyer, upgrader, investor, NRI) and one question they have.
  2. Pick the channel where that buyer is: search for intent, Meta and reels for discovery, hoardings and print for local launches.
  3. Write the answer in one line using a formula above.
  4. Show something real: site footage, the sample flat, the approach road.
  5. Add the RERA number, state RERA website and QR code where required.
  6. Choose one action and make sure someone answers it within minutes.
  7. Measure on site visits per ad, not likes or cost per lead.

To see what other projects run, search Meta's public Ad Library and Google's Ads Transparency Center by advertiser name. Use them for ideas on format, never copy their wording or designs.

What are common real estate ad mistakes?

  • Renders only, with no real photos or video of the site.
  • "Price on request", which attracts curiosity instead of buyers.
  • Ten amenities on one creative and nothing about location.
  • RERA number missing, tiny or hidden in hashtags.
  • Claims the RERA filing doesn't support, such as an earlier possession date or "assured returns".
  • Every ad sent to the same generic page.
  • Judging ads on clicks or cost per lead instead of site visits.
