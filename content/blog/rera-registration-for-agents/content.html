To get RERA agent registration, apply online to the RERA authority of the state where you will sell, with your photograph, PAN, address proof of your place of business, income tax returns for the last three years and the state fee. Some states now also ask for a training certificate first. Once approved, you get an agent registration number that you must quote in every deal and ad.

If you help people buy or sell units in RERA-registered projects, the law expects you to be registered too. Without it you can't legally facilitate a sale in a registered project, most developers won't empanel you, and you risk a daily penalty. This guide covers who needs to register, the documents required, fees and renewal in the states we could confirm on official RERA portals, the online process step by step, the new training rules, and what changes once you have your number.

Who needs RERA registration as an agent?

Anyone who earns a fee or commission for helping sell a plot, apartment or building in a project that must be registered under RERA needs agent registration. Section 2(zm) of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 defines a real estate agent broadly, and the official RERA FAQ published by UP RERA notes that the definition is wide enough to cover web portals that sell plots or apartments.

Section 9(1) of the Act says no agent may facilitate the sale or purchase of, or act on behalf of anyone in, a unit in a project that must be registered, unless the agent is registered with the state authority. In practice:

  • Independent brokers and property consultants who sell units in registered projects need it.
  • Channel partners need it, and developers ask for the number before they empanel you. See our guide to the channel partner in real estate.
  • Firms, LLPs and companies register as an agent "other than individual", with their own documents and a higher fee.
  • Agents working in more than one state need a registration in each one. Section 9(3) gives a single registration for the whole state, and the MahaRERA FAQ confirms a registration is valid only in that state and is not transferable.

So a UP RERA number covers Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Lucknow and Kanpur, but a Gurugram project falls under Haryana RERA and a Delhi project under Delhi RERA. If you're unsure which authority a project falls under, a quick RERA number check will tell you.

What are the advantages of RERA registration for an agent?

The main advantage is that you can legally sell registered projects and claim your commission. Beyond that, registration helps your business in several practical ways:

  • Developer empanelment. Developers check your agent number before signing a channel partner agreement.
  • Public listing. Section 34 of the Act requires each authority to publish details of registered agents on its website, so buyers can look you up and confirm you're genuine.
  • Trust in your marketing. An agent number on your ads, landing pages and visiting card is easy for a buyer to verify.
  • A stronger position in disputes. A registered agent with proper records is better placed in any commission or ownership dispute than one working outside the rules.
  • No penalty risk. Working unregistered exposes you to a daily penalty under Section 62 (covered below).

How to become a real estate agent in India

India has no national real estate licence. State RERA registration is the legal requirement, and a few states now add a training certificate. A practical path:

  1. Pick your market: one or two localities and price bands you can know better than anyone.
  2. Choose your model: resale and rentals as an independent broker, joining a brokerage, or selling new projects as a channel partner. Many agents mix these.
  3. Complete any training your state requires (UP and Maharashtra do; see below).
  4. Register with your state RERA as an individual, or as a firm if you have partners.
  5. Sort out tax registration. Brokerage is a taxable service, and GST registration becomes compulsory once your turnover crosses the GST threshold. Ask a CA what applies to you.
  6. Get empanelled with developers, who check your RERA number, PAN and GST details before signing a CP agreement (see real estate broker commission in India).
  7. Build your own lead source. Portal leads are shared with other brokers (our 99acres vs MagicBricks comparison explains why), so learn how to generate real estate leads of your own.

Do you need a training certificate before registering?

In some states, yes. Two of the biggest markets now link agent registration to a training and exam:

  • Uttar Pradesh: UP RERA runs a four-day agent training programme in Lucknow and Greater Noida, followed by a certification exam. According to UP RERA's user manual for agent registration, the fee is ₹6,000 per person enrolled, only those who attend all four days can sit the exam, and the registration form opens on your dashboard only after your training certificate is uploaded. Already-registered agents have also been told to complete the training (The Realty Today).
  • Maharashtra: MahaRERA requires a Certificate of Competency, earned through training and an exam, for new registration and for renewal (Housing.com).

Other states may add similar rules, so check your state portal's notices before you apply.

How to apply for RERA agent registration online, step by step

Online RERA registration for agents follows the same pattern in most states, even though the screens differ:

  1. Complete any required training and keep the certificate ready (UP, Maharashtra).
  2. Open your state RERA portal and choose agent registration: up-rera.in, hareraggm.gov.in or haryanarera.gov.in, maharera.maharashtra.gov.in.
  3. Sign up with your mobile number and email, choosing individual or "other than individual". This becomes your login and contact on record.
  4. Fill in the application form (Form G under the UP rules, Form REA-I in Haryana) with your personal or firm details and business address.
  5. Upload your documents in the formats and sizes the portal asks for.
  6. Pay the fee online or by demand draft, as the portal allows, and keep the receipt.
  7. Submit any physical copy the authority asks for. HARERA Gurugram, for example, asks for one printed set of the form and documents at its reception.
  8. Answer queries raised on the portal or by email promptly.
  9. Download your certificate with your agent registration number. HARERA Gurugram says it issues the certificate within 30 days once documents are found complete.

Under Section 9(4) of the Act, if the authority doesn't grant or reject your application within the period set in the state's rules, you are deemed registered. A rejection must give reasons, after giving you a chance to be heard.

Why do RERA agent applications get delayed?

Most delays come from small, avoidable mistakes:

  • Name mismatches between PAN, the form and other IDs.
  • Unclear or wrong address proof. It must be for your place of business, legible and authenticated.
  • Applying as the wrong type. Two partners running one business may need a partnership or LLP application, not two individual ones. Decide before you pay, because the fee differs.
  • Missing returns. If you were exempt from filing for some years, give the declaration the rules allow instead of leaving it blank.
  • Missing training certificate in states that require it.
  • Ignoring the portal. An unanswered query can hold the application for weeks.

What are an agent's duties after registration (Section 10)?

Section 10 of the Act says a registered agent must not facilitate the sale of unregistered projects, must keep books of account and records as the state rules require, must not use unfair trade practices (false claims about quality, approvals or services), and must help the buyer get the documents they are entitled to at booking. Most state rules also require your registration number in every sale you facilitate.

Section 9(7) lets the authority revoke a registration that was obtained through misrepresentation or fraud, or whose conditions are breached. UP RERA has removed agents with lapsed registrations from project records (Realty N More), so renewal matters as much as the first registration.

Good records also protect your commission. A timestamped log of when each buyer first contacted you and when you registered them with the developer is your best evidence in an ownership dispute. Our channel partner lead registration playbook shows a simple way to set this up.

What is the penalty for working without RERA registration?

Under Section 62 of the Act, an agent who fails to register or breaks the Section 9 or 10 rules can be fined up to ₹10,000 for every day of default, adding up to as much as 5% of the cost of the plot, apartment or building whose sale was facilitated (UP RERA FAQ). Two more sections apply after an order is made:

  • Section 65: not following an order of the authority, a daily penalty that can add up to 5% of the unit's cost.
  • Section 66: not following an order of the Appellate Tribunal, a daily penalty up to 10% of the unit's cost, or imprisonment up to one year, or both.

As an illustration of the 5% ceiling: on a ₹60 lakh apartment, it works out to ₹3 lakh for that one deal.

How do real estate agents earn in India?

Agents earn commission, not a salary, and RERA doesn't fix its size. In resale and rentals, brokerage is agreed in writing with the buyer, seller or both. In new projects, the developer pays the commission set in your channel partner agreement, sometimes with volume slabs. Check whether payout depends on the buyer's EOI in real estate turning into a booking. Your income depends on two things you control: how many site visits you generate, and how many you close.
