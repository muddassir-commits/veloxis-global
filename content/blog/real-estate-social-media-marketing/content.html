What does social media actually do for real estate?

Social media gives a property business proof, memory and warm audiences for ads; on its own it rarely produces a steady flow of enquiries. Buying a home takes months and involves the family, so one post seldom leads straight to a booking.

  • Proof. Before a buyer calls, they look you up. Real site photos, construction updates and the team's faces show you exist and the project is moving.
  • Memory. A buyer who went quiet three months ago keeps seeing your posts, so you are the name they remember when they are ready.
  • Warm audiences. People who watched your videos or visited your profile can be shown ads later, which makes paid campaigns better targeted.

Treat organic posting as the base and paid ads as the engine. Most accounts fail by doing neither: posting a rendered tower with "Launching Soon" and a phone number, which is an ad nobody paid to show.

How do you set up a real estate Facebook page and Instagram profile?

Use a searchable name, a one-line bio saying what you sell and where, your RERA details, one clear action and pinned answers to buyer questions.

  1. Name and handle buyers would search. Avoid "official_realty_9876".
  2. Profile photo: a clean logo for a company, a clear face for a broker.
  3. Bio: "2 and 3 BHK homes in Sector 150, Noida. Site visits every day."
  4. RERA details: project or agent registration number and the state authority's website.
  5. One action: a WhatsApp button or one project link.
  6. Pinned posts or highlights: location, floor plans, progress, FAQs.
  7. Professional account connected to your Facebook Page in Meta Business Suite, for insights, one shared inbox and ads.

How do you make real estate posts people watch?

Lead with the facts, film the real place vertically, put text on screen and end with one action.

  • Lead with the answer: "3 BHK, 1,450 sq ft carpet, 8 minutes from the metro" beats "Welcome to a new lifestyle" (use your project's real figures).
  • Film real places. Phone footage of the actual site and sample flat feels real. Label renders as artistic impressions.
  • Vertical first: 9:16 for Reels and stories, 4:5 for feed posts and carousels.
  • Text on screen for people watching with sound off.
  • Carousels to explain a floor plan, a cost breakdown or "5 questions to ask before booking".
  • One action per post: "Comment PLAN for floor plans" or "Tap WhatsApp to book a Sunday visit".

What is the 5-3-1 rule for real estate social media?

The 5-3-1 rule is an informal rule of thumb, not an official standard: of every nine posts, five give value, three build connection and one sells. You will see other versions of it. The point is balance: an account that only sells gets ignored, and one that never sells gets no enquiries.

How does real estate social media advertising work?

Real estate social media advertising means paying Meta (Facebook and Instagram), YouTube or LinkedIn to show your posts or ads to chosen audiences, usually with a lead form, a WhatsApp chat or a landing page as the goal.

  1. Start with what works organically. Boost or turn into ads the posts and Reels that already earn saves, shares and enquiries.
  2. Choose the destination: an instant lead form, a click-to-WhatsApp ad, or a project landing page.
  3. Follow housing ad rules. Meta's special ad category for housing limits some targeting options for housing ads where it applies (Meta for Developers); details for India are in Meta ads for real estate in India.
  4. Retarget people who watched your videos or visited your page; see real estate retargeting ads.
  5. Measure cost per site visit, not cost per lead, and move budget to what produces visits.

Do real estate social media posts need a RERA number?

Yes, if they promote a project. Section 11(2) of the RERA Act, 2016 requires a promoter's advertisements to prominently mention the authority's website and the project's registration details. A Reel promoting a project, a Facebook launch post and a WhatsApp creative are all advertisements. Put the number on the creative itself, because images get forwarded without captions. Some authorities also require a QR code: MahaRERA since August 2023 (The Realty Today) and UP RERA since July 2024 (The Week).

  • Never promote an unregistered project unless it clearly falls under an exemption.
  • Mark renders and sample flat photos as artistic impressions.
  • Don't promise returns, rental yields or appreciation you can't document.
  • Use buyer photos and names only with written permission.

More in RERA advertisement rules. General information, not legal advice; rules checked 5 November 2026. Check your state authority's latest orders.

How do you turn comments and DMs into site visits?

Reply within minutes from one inbox, move the chat to WhatsApp, ask two qualifying questions, offer a specific visit slot and log where the lead came from. A buyer who comments "price?" and hears back the next afternoon has already messaged other projects.

  1. One inbox: comments, DMs and Facebook messages land in the Meta Business Suite inbox; give one person the job of checking it through the day.
  2. Short public reply, then send the price sheet and floor plans on WhatsApp.
  3. Two questions: budget and timeline, or configuration and preferred visit day. See lead qualification questions.
  4. Offer a specific slot: "Saturday 11 am or Sunday 4 pm?" Our guide to booking site visits has scripts.
  5. Log the source post or Reel in your sheet or CRM.

WhatsApp automation can send the brochure and a visit slot within seconds, even late at night, then hand over to a person.

How do you measure real estate social media marketing?

Measure enquiries and site visits from social, not likes or followers. Once a month, track:

  • Enquiries from social: DMs, detail-request comments, WhatsApp chats started from a post.
  • Site visits from social: the number that matters.
  • Saves and shares: content useful enough to send to a spouse or parent.
  • Profile visits and link clicks.
  • Which posts led to enquiries, so you make more of them.
