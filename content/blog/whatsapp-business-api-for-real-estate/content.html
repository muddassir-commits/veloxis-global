The WhatsApp Business API (officially the WhatsApp Business Platform) lets a real estate business connect WhatsApp to its ads, website and CRM, so every enquiry gets an instant reply, buyers are qualified automatically, and site visit confirmations and reminders go out without anyone typing them. It is not free: Meta charges per delivered message by category. And it is not a licence to bulk-message people who never asked to hear from you.

This guide explains how the API differs from the WhatsApp Business app, what it costs under Meta's current pricing, what a real estate WhatsApp flow looks like, how chatbots fit in, and the rules on templates, opt-in and bulk messages.

Is the WhatsApp Business API free?

No. According to Meta's pricing documentation:

  • Meta charges per delivered message, based on the message category (marketing, utility, authentication, service) and the recipient's country.
  • Messages from users to your business are not charged.
  • From 1 October 2026, service messages (your free-form replies inside the 24-hour customer service window) are charged, with the first 1,000 delivered service messages per business phone number per month free. Utility messages sent within an open service window are also charged from that date.
  • Messages sent within a free entry point window, opened when a user messages you from a click-to-WhatsApp ad or a Facebook Page call-to-action button, are not charged.
  • Marketing messages cost the most.

On top of Meta's charges, the business solution provider (the company whose dashboard you use) charges its own fee. Check Meta's current rate card for India before budgeting, because rates and rules change.

A real estate WhatsApp flow, step by step

  1. Enquiry arrives from a Meta lead form, a Google ad, the website form or a click-to-WhatsApp ad.
  2. Instant reply: the project name, the brochure, the price band and a question with buttons: "2 BHK / 3 BHK?"
  3. Qualifying questions: budget range, timeline and purpose, each a tap rather than typing.
  4. Visit slots: two specific times offered as buttons.
  5. Handover to a person: the salesperson gets an alert with the answers and calls with context.
  6. Confirmation and reminders: location pin and host's number on booking, a reminder the day before and the morning of the visit.
  7. After the visit: thanks, the cost sheet for the unit they liked, and the next step.

Each step outside a 24-hour window opened by the buyer needs an approved template. Write templates that name the project and the buyer and give useful information. "Sir, you enquired about our project?" gives no reason to reply.

Can WhatsApp chatbots be used for real estate?

Yes. A WhatsApp chatbot for real estate can answer common questions (price band, configurations, location, amenities, RERA number), send brochures and floor plans, collect qualifying answers and book visits, at any hour. What it shouldn't do is invent: a bot must only answer from approved project information, and should hand over to a person for anything about pricing negotiations, legal questions or complaints. Can you send bulk WhatsApp messages to property buyers?

Only to people who opted in to hear from you on WhatsApp. The WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy requires opt-in before you message people, and business-initiated messages must use approved templates. Meta also sets messaging limits and a quality rating for every number. If many recipients block or report you, your limits and eventually the number itself are restricted.

So a bought list of phone numbers is a fast way to lose a WhatsApp number. It also conflicts with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, which requires a lawful basis, usually consent, to use someone's personal data. Broadcasts work when they go to past enquirers and buyers who agreed to updates, and carry real news: construction progress, a new tower, a site event.

Using WhatsApp to reduce site visit no-shows

  • Confirm the visit immediately with the date, time, location pin and the host's name and number.
  • Send a reminder the evening before and on the morning of the visit, with buttons to confirm or change the time.
  • If they don't show, send a friendly message the same day with two new slots.

How to set up the WhatsApp Business API

  1. Choose a business solution provider (or use Meta's Cloud API directly if you have a developer).
  2. Verify your business in Meta Business Manager.
  3. Register a phone number that isn't already active on the WhatsApp app (or migrate it).
  4. Submit message templates for approval: welcome, visit confirmation, reminder, follow-up.
  5. Connect lead sources (Meta lead forms, website, Google ads) and your CRM.
  6. Build the flow, test it end to end, and train the sales team on the handover.
