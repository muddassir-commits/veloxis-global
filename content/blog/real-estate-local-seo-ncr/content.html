A Google Business Profile for a real estate agent is a free listing that puts your agency in Google Maps and the local results for searches like "property dealer near me". To rank, verify it, use your real business name, pick "Real estate agency" or "Real estate agent" as the category, set an honest service area, add real photos and collect genuine reviews.

This guide is for brokers, property dealers, channel partners (CPs) and small developers in India, with examples from Delhi NCR, Lucknow and Kanpur. It covers who is eligible, the setup step by step, the description, office address versus service area, photos and posts, reviews, citations, tracking and a weekly routine. Every rule links to Google's own help pages.

Why does a Google Business Profile matter for real estate agents?

Because local searches show map results with Business Profiles, and buyers often check your profile before they call. Google says local results are based mainly on three things (Google Business Profile Help):

  • Relevance: how well your profile matches the search. Categories, services, description and reviews feed this.
  • Distance: how far you are from the searcher, or from the place named in the search.
  • Prominence: how well known you are, including links, mentions, and your review count and rating.

You can't change distance, but you can improve relevance and prominence. Google is also clear that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. People who hear your name from a developer or past client often search for you before calling, so a complete profile with real photos and genuine reviews is your first impression.

Can a real estate agent have a Google Business Profile, and is it free?

Yes. A Business Profile is free, and a real estate agent or agency that meets clients in person qualifies. Google's guidelines for representing your business name real estate agents as an example of individual practitioners: public-facing professionals with their own clients.

The rules that matter most for property businesses:

  • No virtual offices. A rented address where you don't actually work isn't eligible, and a co-working desk needs clear signage of its own.
  • One profile per real location. Don't create profiles for localities where you have no staffed office; this is a common reason for suspension.
  • Agents inside an agency can have their own profile if they deal with the public directly; support staff should not.
  • A project sales office can be listed only if it is staffed during the hours shown, named as the developer's sales office rather than a keyword like "best flats".

How do I set up a Google Business Profile for real estate?

Create the profile at Google Business Profile, enter your real name and category, add your contact details and services, then complete verification. Step by step:

  1. Name: your real business name exactly as on your signboard and documents. Google doesn't permit extra information in the name, so "Sharma Realty – Best Flats in Noida" breaks the rules and risks suspension.
  2. Primary category: "Real estate agency" or "Real estate agent". Google asks you to use as few categories as possible to describe your core business. Add "Real estate consultant" only if it fits, and "Real estate developer" only if you are one.
  3. Phone and website: the same number and website you use everywhere else. A WhatsApp-enabled number helps, since many buyers prefer to chat.
  4. Services: what you really handle: new projects, resale, plots, commercial, rentals, NRI assistance.
  5. Hours: real hours, including Sundays if you do weekend site visits, with special hours for festivals.
  6. Verification: complete it. Google says verification makes a profile more likely to show in results (Google), and verification can be by phone, email, live video call, video recording or postcard, depending on what Google offers you (Google verification help).

How do I write a real estate business description?

Use up to 750 characters to say who you are and where, what property you handle, one true reason to choose you, and what to do next. Google sets the 750-character limit and asks you to focus on your business rather than promotions or prices (Google), and its guidelines say no links of any kind are allowed (Google).

Should a real estate agent show the office address or a service area?

Show the address if clients visit your office; if you meet clients only at sites, set up as a service-area business, hide the address and list the areas you cover. Google's guidelines say service-area businesses should hide their address, and can't list a virtual office unless it is staffed during business hours (Google). Hybrid businesses can show an address and a service area (Google).

Keep the service area realistic: the sectors or localities you actually work in. A profile claiming all of Delhi NCR is less relevant to each locality than one that honestly covers Sector 150, Sector 143 and Noida Extension.

What photos and posts should a real estate profile have?

Real photos of your office, team and the projects you are authorised to sell, plus regular posts about launches, site-visit days and locality news. Google says photos and videos let you show customers what you offer (Google).

  • Photos: office front with signage, team, sample flats and site progress. No stock images; add new ones regularly.
  • Short videos: a phone walkthrough of a sample flat or a site visit.
  • Posts: new launches, site-visit weekends and updates such as a new metro station opening. Link each to the matching project landing page.
  • Questions: answer the questions buyers ask ("Do you charge brokerage on new projects?") in your description, posts or website FAQ.

Ads and offers must still follow RERA rules: show the project registration number where required. See RERA advertisement rules.

How do real estate agents get more Google reviews?

Ask every client for an honest review right after something went well, with a direct review link sent on WhatsApp, and reply to every review. Google's policies prohibit fake engagement, including incentivised reviews and selectively asking only happy customers (Google Maps content policy). Breaking them can remove reviews or suspend the profile.

  1. Copy your review link from the profile ("Ask for reviews").
  2. Send it personally after a site visit, booking or registration.
  3. Never offer gifts or discounts in return, and never write reviews yourself.
  4. Reply to each review. For complaints, thank the person, address the issue and offer to continue offline. Google notes that replying shows you value feedback (Google).

What are local citations for real estate?

Citations are mentions of your business name, address and phone (NAP) on other websites, and they should match your profile exactly. Links and mentions across the web feed prominence (Google), and matching details make it clear they are all the same business. For an Indian agent, the important ones are:

  • Your own website's contact page and footer.
  • Agent profiles on property portals such as 99acres, MagicBricks and Housing.com (compared in 99acres vs MagicBricks).
  • Directories such as Justdial and Sulekha.
  • Your state RERA agent registration.
  • LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram pages.

If you moved office or changed numbers, update the old listings. Conflicting details create doubt about which is correct.

What should the profile link to?

Link to a page that explains what you do, the localities you cover and the projects you handle, with WhatsApp and call buttons. For project posts, link the specific project page instead of your homepage. A locality page with real information (connectivity, price bands, projects you handle) gives Google another relevance signal and gives buyers a reason to contact you rather than a portal. How do I track leads from a Google Business Profile?

Use the profile's Performance view for calls, chats, directions and website clicks, tag the website link with UTM parameters, and ask every enquiry how they found you.

  • UTM link: for example ?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp, so profile visits show separately in Google Analytics (GA4 help on UTM parameters).
  • Source field: record "Google Maps" in your CRM or lead sheet. Profile leads often call directly and go uncounted otherwise.

What is a good weekly Google Business Profile routine?

Thirty minutes a week is enough to keep a real estate profile active and accurate.

  1. Publish one post: a project update, site-visit weekend or locality news.
  2. Add two or three new real photos.
  3. Reply to every new review.
  4. Send review links to the clients you helped that week.
  5. Check hours, phone and services are still correct.
