What are Instagram Reels and how does Instagram rank them?

Reels are Instagram's vertical videos, up to three minutes long, shown full-screen in the Reels tab, the feed, Explore and your profile. Instagram raised the limit from 90 seconds to three minutes in January 2025 (Social Media Today). Unlike a normal post, a Reel is often shown to people who don't follow you, which is why a new account can still reach beyond its followers.

Instagram's own ranking explainer says Reels are ranked using your activity (Reels you liked, saved, reshared and commented on), your history with the poster, information about the Reel such as its audio and visuals, and information about the poster. It names the likelihood that you will reshare a Reel as one of the most important predictions. It also says low-resolution or watermarked Reels, muted Reels and Reels with borders are made less visible, so a video exported from another app with its logo on it starts at a disadvantage.

In practice: make Reels people want to send to someone ("look at this view", "this is what we discussed"), film them natively, and keep them sharp and clean.

Why do Reels work for business, and where don't they?

Reels are good at reach, proof and building audiences for ads; on their own they don't produce a predictable flow of enquiries.

  • Reach: local people who don't follow you yet.
  • Proof: before anyone enquires, they look you up. A profile of real videos of real places, with your face and voice, shows you exist and know the market.
  • Warm audiences: people who watched your Reels can be shown ads later.

Views rise and fall, and many viewers are curious rather than ready to buy. Treat Reels as the top of the funnel and pair them with ads and fast follow-up. The wider posting plan is in our guide to real estate social media marketing.

How should you set up your account before posting Reels?

Switch to a professional account and make your profile answer "who, where, what next" in three seconds, because a viewer who likes your Reel taps your name next.

  1. Professional account: Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account → Business or Creator. This unlocks insights, contact buttons and boosting.
  2. Name field with a keyword: "Rahul Verma | Noida Property Advisor" is easier to find by searching "Noida property" than "Rahul Verma" alone.
  3. Bio: who you help, where, and what to do next. "2 & 3 BHK flats in Noida Extension · RERA-registered projects only · WhatsApp for prices and site visits".
  4. WhatsApp button: Edit profile → Add action buttons → WhatsApp.
  5. One working link: a project page or a short link page.
  6. Story highlights for Projects, Site visits, Possession, FAQ.

What are the best real estate Reel ideas?

The best real estate Reels answer a question buyers already have or show something a brochure can't. Pick the ideas that fit and repeat the ones that work.

Show the real thing

  1. The 20-second walkthrough: door to balcony in one take, real light, the view at the end.
  2. "What this budget gets you in [locality]": show the flat, say the size and price band on screen, RERA number in the caption.
  3. Construction progress: the same spot filmed every month.
  4. The view from a high floor at sunset: one shot, no talking; the kind of thing people send to family.
  5. Before and after: empty plot to finished building, bare flat to sample flat.

Answer questions

  1. "3 questions to ask before booking a flat."
  2. Explain a confusing term: carpet area vs super built-up, what EOI means, how to check a RERA number.
  3. Reply to a comment with a Reel; the original comment appears on screen.
  4. The total cost breakdown: base price, parking, club, registry, GST.
  5. Myth vs fact about your local market.

Show the people and the place

  1. A day of site visits cut to 20 seconds.
  2. Locality tour: metro, school, hospital, market, each with an honest drive time.
  3. Meet the team: the person who will answer the buyer's WhatsApp.
  4. A handover, only with the buyer's written permission and no personal details on screen.
  5. Trends with a twist, only when you can tie the trend to the property naturally.

How do you film Reels on a phone?

A recent phone, a tripod, a clip-on microphone and good light are enough.

  • Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920 or higher. Clean the lens first.
  • Light from the front: face a window; open every curtain and switch on every light in a flat.
  • Sound matters: viewers forgive slightly shaky video more than bad audio. Use a clip-on mic when you talk.
  • Move slowly at half walking speed, phone at chest height, or use a gimbal.
  • Short clips of three to five seconds with a moment of stillness at each end.
  • Keep the edges clear: captions sit at the bottom and buttons on the right, so keep faces and text centred.

How do you write hooks, captions and choose audio?

Open with the result, the buyer's question or the middle of the action, repeat the hook as on-screen text, and end with one clear next step.

  • Result first: "This is a ready-to-move 3 BHK in Noida Extension."
  • The buyer's question: "Is under-construction still worth it?"
  • Mid-action: the door opening, the view appearing, keys handed over.
  • On-screen text, since many people watch with sound off.

Instagram's editor, the Edits app or CapCut are enough. Cut every pause, caption every spoken word and end with "WhatsApp FLAT for the price list" rather than "link in bio". In the caption, say what and where in plain words ("2 BHK ready-to-move flat in Gomti Nagar, Lucknow") and use a few specific hashtags. For music, business accounts should use sounds licensed for commercial use, such as Meta's royalty-free Sound Collection; a copyrighted film song can get a Reel muted or removed. Closing lines can come from your slogans and taglines.

How do Instagram Reel ads work?

Instagram Reel ads are paid videos that appear between organic Reels in the full-screen Reels viewer; you choose the Reels placement in Meta Ads Manager or boost a Reel that is already performing. Meta says Reels ads show once someone opens the full-screen Reels viewer, and that Advantage+ placements include Reels automatically (Meta Reels ads).

  1. Start from a proven organic Reel with strong watch time and shares, or edit a new 9:16 video with sound.
  2. Keep text and the logo in the safe zone. For Reels images Meta suggests leaving roughly the top 14% and bottom 35% free of text or logos (Meta ads guide); use the safe-zone preview in Ads Manager for video.
  3. Pick the goal: leads (instant form), messages (click-to-WhatsApp) or traffic to a project landing page.
  4. Target your area and buyers, and remember housing ads fall under Meta's special ad category rules; see Meta ads for real estate in India.
  5. Retarget viewers: build an audience of people who watched your Reels and show them the project ad. More in real estate retargeting ads.
  6. Judge by cost per site visit, not cost per view.

What rules apply to real estate Reels?

A Reel promoting a specific project is an advertisement, so RERA rules apply as they do to a newspaper ad or hoarding.

  • Show the RERA registration number of the project, and your agent number if you are a broker. In Maharashtra, MahaRERA has also required a QR code in project advertisements since 1 August 2023 (Housing.com News). State rules are in RERA advertisement rules.
  • No unprovable promises: no "guaranteed returns", no possession date that differs from the RERA filing.
  • Label renders as artist's impressions.
  • Licensed music only.
  • Privacy: written permission from anyone clearly on camera; never show a buyer's name, flat number or documents.

How do you turn Reel views into enquiries?

Give one action in every Reel, reply to comments and DMs within minutes, and move the conversation to WhatsApp to book a site visit. Most leads are lost in the step after the Reel, not in the Reel.

  1. One clear action: "Comment PRICE" or "WhatsApp FLAT" at the end and in the caption.
  2. Fast replies: an instant automatic reply on Instagram and WhatsApp, then a person. See WhatsApp automation for real estate leads.
  3. Move to WhatsApp and offer two site-visit times, not "interested?".
  4. Boost or run ads behind Reels that already work.
  5. Log the source of every enquiry in your sheet or CRM.

Which Reel metrics should you track?

Track reach (especially non-followers), watch time, shares and saves, profile actions, and the enquiries and site visits each Reel produces. Check Insights after 48 hours and after a week:

  • Accounts reached and share of non-followers: is Instagram showing it to new people?
  • Average watch time and skip rate: if most leave early, fix the hook.
  • Shares and saves: the clearest sign it was useful, and resharing is a key ranking prediction.
  • Profile visits, follows and WhatsApp taps.
  • Enquiries and site visits that mention the Reel.

After a month, sort Reels by enquiries, not views. Make more of the top formats and drop the weakest.

What are common Instagram Reels mistakes?

  • Posting ads as Reels: a render with "Launching soon" and a phone number.
  • Slow starts: logos, drone intros and greetings before the point.
  • No captions for sound-off viewers.
  • Reposting videos with another app's watermark, which Instagram says it shows less.
  • Copyrighted music on a business account.
  • Missing RERA details on project Reels.
  • Slow replies that waste the attention the Reel earned.
  • Giving up after a few weeks. Reach takes steady posting to build.
