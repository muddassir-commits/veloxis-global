Google Ads for real estate works best as a Search campaign on project-name, locality and configuration keywords, with a long negative keyword list, "Presence" location targeting and conversion tracking that reports site visits, not just form fills. Add Performance Max only after tracking is solid, and send every click to a project landing page.

When someone types "3 BHK in Sector 150 Noida", "ready to move flats in Gomti Nagar" or your project's name into Google, they have already decided what they want. This guide is for developers, brokers and channel partners running Google ads (Google PPC) for real estate in India, or checking whether their agency's real estate Google Ads management is set up properly. It covers keywords by intent, campaign settings, Performance Max vs Search, negative keywords, ad copy, tracking and budget.

Do Google Ads work for real estate in India?

Yes, when people are already searching. Google Search reaches buyers at the moment they type what they want, which gives you three advantages:

  • Intent. Someone searching a locality, configuration or project name is further along than someone scrolling Instagram.
  • Exclusive leads. Unlike portal enquiries, a lead from your own ad comes only to you.
  • Control. You choose the searches you appear for, the areas you target and what you are willing to pay.

The limit is demand: Google can only show your ad when people search. For a brand-new launch with an unknown name, Meta usually builds interest first. See Meta ads for real estate in India and our Google vs Meta comparison.

Performance Max vs Search: which should a real estate project use?

Search first, Performance Max second. Performance Max uses Google's automation to find conversions across all of Google's channels from one campaign. That is powerful when it optimises for the right thing, and costly when it optimises for any form fill, because it will find the cheapest people willing to fill forms.

  • Run Search alone while you collect your first qualified leads and site visits.
  • Add Performance Max once qualified leads or site visits are reported back as conversions (see tracking below).
  • Add negative keywords to Performance Max. Google now supports campaign-level negative keywords in Performance Max, which apply to Search and Shopping inventory, so your rent and job exclusions can apply there too.
  • Keep brand terms in a Search campaign so you can see what your project-name searches cost.

Which negative keywords should real estate ads use?

Negative keywords save the most money, because property searches attract many people who will never buy a new flat. Add a list before launch and check the search terms report every week:

  • Renting: rent, rental, PG, hostel, lease, roommate, flatmate
  • Jobs and study: job, jobs, salary, vacancy, career, course, internship
  • Wrong stage: resale (for new launches), old, second hand, auction, bank auction
  • Information seekers: news, map, meaning, pdf, free, wiki, case, complaint
  • Other uses: hotel, banquet, guest house, construction material, interior
  • Portals by name, if you don't want people looking for a specific portal

Google's negative keywords help page explains how negative match types differ from normal ones; for example, negative keywords don't match close variants, so add plurals and common spellings yourself.

Which location setting should real estate ads use?

For most projects, "Presence". Google's location options page says "Presence or interest" is the default and recommended option, and it also reaches people who have only shown interest in your area. For a local project, choose "Presence: people in or regularly in your included locations" and target the city and nearby areas your buyers come from.

Use "Presence or interest" deliberately when you want buyers from elsewhere, for example NRI buyers searching for property in your city, in a separate campaign with its own budget and ads.

Which campaign settings matter most?

  • Networks: untick the Display Network in Search campaigns, so search budget stays on search.
  • Ad schedule: run all day if WhatsApp replies are automatic; otherwise lower bids when nobody can call back.
  • Languages: include English and Hindi (or your local language) for India.
  • Bidding: start with Maximise conversions, then move to a target cost per acquisition once there is enough conversion data, as Smart Bidding learns from it.
  • Assets: sitelinks, callouts, a call asset and, if you want enquiries without a page visit, a lead form asset.

How do you write Google search ads for property?

A responsive search ad can have up to 15 headlines of up to 30 characters and 4 descriptions of up to 90 characters, according to Google Ads Help. Use them well:

  • Match the search: the project ad group's ad names the project; the locality ad group's ad names the locality.
  • Price band and configuration: "2 & 3 BHK from ₹X Lakh*".
  • Trust: "RERA Registered", possession date as filed with RERA, the developer's name.
  • One clear action: "Get the Price Sheet on WhatsApp", "Book a Site Visit".

Every ad must stay consistent with the RERA registration, and the landing page should show the registration number, state RERA website and, where required, the QR code. See RERA advertisement rules and our real estate ad examples for search ad copy patterns.

Where should Google Ads clicks land?

On a project landing page, never the homepage or a portal listing. The page should repeat what the ad promised (project, locality, price band, RERA details), have a short form and a WhatsApp button, and load fast on a phone. Fast, relevant pages also help your Quality Score. Our landing page checklist covers what to include.

How do you track site visits from Google Ads?

  1. Track every enquiry as a conversion: form submissions, calls from ads, WhatsApp clicks and lead form assets.
  2. Store the click ID (GCLID) with each lead in your CRM.
  3. Mark lead stages in the CRM: qualified, site visit booked, site visit done, booking.
  4. Send those stages back with enhanced conversions for leads, which Google describes as an upgraded version of offline conversion import using hashed, user-provided data such as email or phone.
  5. Bid on the deeper goal: once enough site visits are reported, make "qualified lead" or "site visit" the primary conversion, rather than every form fill.

A real estate CRM that records the source and stage of every lead makes steps 2 to 4 possible.

How much do Google Ads cost for real estate in India?

It depends on city, locality, keyword and competition, because Google runs an auction for every search. We have not found a public, sourced cost-per-click or cost-per-lead benchmark for Indian real estate, so be careful with figures quoted online without a source. Use Google's Keyword Planner to see bid ranges for your own keywords, give each campaign enough budget to collect data (a campaign that gets a handful of clicks a day can't learn), and judge it on cost per site visit, not clicks or leads. Common Google Ads mistakes in real estate

  • Default "Presence or interest" location, showing ads to people far from the project.
  • No negative keywords, so renters and job-seekers eat the budget.
  • Broad match with no conversion data.
  • Performance Max optimising for form fills before qualified leads are tracked.
  • Clicks sent to the homepage.
  • Leads called the next day instead of within minutes.
