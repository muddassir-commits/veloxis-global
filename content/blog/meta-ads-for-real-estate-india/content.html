Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram ads) work for real estate in India when you judge them by site visits, not by cheap leads. Run a lead form with qualifying questions or send people to a fast project landing page, show the real project, locality and price band, reply on WhatsApp within minutes, and retarget people who watched your videos or opened the form.

This guide is for builders, developers, channel partners and brokers who run Facebook ads for property listings or new projects, or who are deciding whether to hire a real estate Facebook ads service. It covers which campaign to pick, whether Meta's housing rules apply in India, targeting, lead forms, creatives, a step-by-step setup, cost and how to measure it.

Do Facebook ads work for real estate in India?

Yes, for the right job. Meta reaches people before they start searching, so it is the main paid channel for jobs where search demand doesn't exist yet:

  • New launches and EOI drives, when nobody is searching for the project name.
  • Retargeting people who visited your project page, watched a walkthrough or opened a form without submitting.
  • Local awareness around a project, with video that shows the site, the approach road and nearby landmarks.
  • NRI buyers abroad who follow property news from their home city but are not searching on Google yet.

Meta is weak when it is judged on cost per lead alone. Instant forms make enquiring effortless, so without filtering you also collect curious scrollers who don't pick up. For buyers who are already searching for a locality or project name, Google Search usually does better; see our Google Ads vs Meta ads comparison. "Meta ads" and "Facebook ads" mean the same thing: Meta is the company, and one Ads Manager account runs ads on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp entry points.

Most real estate accounts need a Leads campaign plus a small retargeting budget. The choice inside "Leads" is where the lead goes next:

Does Meta's Special Ad Category for housing apply in India?

Only if your ads reach certain countries. According to Meta's Special Ad Category documentation, advertisers based in the US or running ads that reach the US, Canada or Europe must declare the housing category for housing ads. Businesses outside the US whose ads don't reach those regions still see the field, but may choose to opt in or out of the audience restrictions.

In practice, for an Indian developer:

  • Ads shown only in India: declaring housing is optional. Many advertisers still keep targeting broad, because the creative does most of the filtering anyway.
  • NRI campaigns that reach the US, Canada or Europe: declare the housing category. Run them as a separate campaign so your India campaigns keep their full options (see our NRI real estate marketing guide).

When the housing category is on, Meta's documentation lists these limits: age is generally fixed at 18 to 65+, you can't choose gender, location exclusion isn't supported and locations need a minimum radius (postcodes and neighbourhoods are not supported), demographic and behaviour targeting and exclusions are not allowed, interests must come from an approved list, and lookalike audiences are unavailable. Google applies similar limits to housing ads in the US and Canada only, under its personalised advertising policy.

General information, not legal advice. Meta's and Google's housing ad rules were checked on 5 Nov 2026 and can change; confirm in Ads Manager before launch.

How should you target Facebook ads for property listings?

Start with geography, then let the creative do the rest. For most Indian projects, targeting means the city, the localities your buyers live and work in, and sometimes NRI markets. A plotted project on Sultanpur Road and an apartment in Gomti Nagar in Lucknow need different localities and different ads (see how we plan real estate campaigns in Lucknow, and our guide on how to sell plots online). Beyond that:

  • Let the creative target. Broad and Advantage+ audiences work best when the ad itself says who it is for: price band, configuration and locality in the first seconds.
  • Retarget your own audiences: website visitors, video viewers and people who opened your lead form but didn't submit. Our real estate retargeting guide covers what to show each one.
  • Exclude existing buyers and people who already enquired, so you don't pay for the same lead twice.
  • Separate ad sets by location and configuration when the price gap is large, so each ad can name the right price band.
  • Brokers with many listings: group listings by locality and budget rather than running one ad per flat. One ad per cluster gives Meta enough data to learn.

How do you set up a lead form that filters out junk leads?

The form is where most lead quality is won or lost. Build it in this order:

  1. Choose the higher-intent form type. It adds a review screen before the person submits, which cuts accidental and low-effort leads.
  2. Show the price band and location on the intro screen. People who can't afford the project leave before submitting, which is exactly what you want.
  3. Ask two or three qualifying questions as multiple choice: budget band, configuration (2, 3 or 4 BHK) and when they plan to buy. Short enough that serious buyers still finish.
  4. Add a consent line and your privacy policy link that explains you will call and message about this project.
  5. Say what happens next on the thank-you screen: "We'll send the price sheet on WhatsApp now." People answer a message they were expecting.
  6. Connect the form to your CRM so every lead lands with its campaign name and is called or messaged at once, not downloaded as a CSV the next morning.

A list of lead qualification questions helps you pick the questions that matter for your project.

What Facebook and Instagram ad creatives work for real estate?

Creatives that answer where, how much, how far and can I trust this builder do the targeting for you:

  • Short walkthrough reels filmed in the sample flat, ending on the price band and a WhatsApp prompt. Reels that already do well organically are often the easiest ads to put money behind; see our Reels guide.
  • Commute videos from the nearest metro station or expressway exit, with a timer on screen.
  • Price-band statics: "2 & 3 BHK in [locality] from ₹X lakh*", with the asterisk explained on the landing page.
  • Construction progress with the month on screen, for under-construction projects.
  • Carousels with one card per unit type, carpet area and floor plan.

Every creative for a registered project must show the RERA registration number and the state RERA website (Section 11(2) of the RERA Act, 2016), and agents should add their own registration number. Some states ask for more: UP RERA asks for the project QR code (PTI, July 2024). Social posts are not exempt: in its half-yearly complaints report for April to September 2024, ASCI named real estate the most violative sector, at 34% of cases investigated. How to set up a Meta ad campaign for a real estate project: step by step

  1. Get the basics ready: a Business Manager, a Facebook Page and Instagram account for the project or brand, a WhatsApp Business number, and the Meta Pixel on your landing page.
  2. Create a Leads campaign in Ads Manager and decide whether the lead goes to an instant form, WhatsApp or your website.
  3. Answer the Special Ad Category question correctly for the countries your ads will reach (see above).
  4. Set the location to the city and localities your buyers come from; keep age and interests broad unless you have a reason.
  5. Upload three to five creatives per ad set: a reel, a static and a carousel, each with the RERA number.
  6. Build the form using the steps above, and test it by submitting a lead yourself.
  7. Turn on instant follow-up: a WhatsApp message with the brochure and price sheet, and a call from the sales team.
  8. Review weekly on contacted, qualified and site-visit numbers from your CRM, and pause what brings no visits.

How do you teach Meta what a good lead looks like?

Send back what happened after the form. By default Meta optimises for whatever you call success; if that is "form submitted", it finds more people who submit forms. Meta's Conversions API for CRM integration lets you report lead stages from your CRM, such as qualified, site visit booked and site visit done, and then optimise for "conversion leads" instead of raw form fills. It takes some weeks of data, but it is one of the biggest improvements most real estate ad accounts can make. A real estate CRM that records the stage of every lead makes this possible.

How fast should you follow up on Facebook leads?

Within minutes, ideally seconds. A Harvard Business Review study of online leads found that firms contacting leads within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to qualify them as firms that waited longer. A Meta lead from 10 pm who gets a call at 11 am has usually spoken to other projects. The best setups reply on WhatsApp straight away with the brochure and price sheet, ask the qualifying questions again in conversation, and offer site-visit slots. How much do Facebook ads cost for real estate in India?

There is no fixed price. Meta runs an auction, so your cost per lead depends on the city, the audience, the season, the creative, the form type and how many other advertisers want the same people. We have not found a public, methodology-backed cost-per-lead benchmark for Indian real estate on Meta, so treat any figure quoted online without a source with caution. Instead, set a test budget, run it for a few weeks, and calculate your own numbers: spend ÷ leads, spend ÷ qualified leads and spend ÷ site visits. Our real estate marketing budget guide shows the calculation step by step. The ad spend is paid directly to Meta.

How should you measure Meta ads for real estate?

On cost per site visit from your CRM, not cost per lead in Ads Manager. Meta leads usually look cheaper than Google leads, which is exactly why cost per lead misleads. Track leads, contacted, qualified, site visits and bookings for each campaign, and cut campaigns that bring enquiries but no visits, however cheap they look.

What should you ask a real estate Facebook ads service or agency?

Ask how they will be judged, not how many leads they promise. Good questions:

  • Will the ad account, Pixel and Page be in our name, so we keep them if we stop working together?
  • Will you report site visits and bookings by campaign, or only leads and cost per lead?
  • How fast is each lead contacted, and by whom?
  • How do you handle RERA numbers, QR codes and the Special Ad Category?
  • Can you show the targeting, creatives and forms you plan before spending?

Be careful with anyone who guarantees a lead count or a cost per lead before seeing your project; nobody controls Meta's auction.
