A real estate sales script is a planned outline of what to say at each stage, from the first call and WhatsApp reply to the site visit invitation, objections, follow-up and asking for the booking, so every salesperson gives buyers clear, consistent answers. It isn't read word for word. It's a structure: open, ask, listen, match, invite. The goal of the first call is to understand the buyer and book a site visit, not to sell the home on the phone.

Below are real estate sales pitch samples and scripts for India: a 30-second pitch, calling a new lead, the first WhatsApp reply, inviting a site visit, handling common objections, following up after a visit, asking for the booking, pitching to a seller, and the rules for cold calling. Several come with a Hinglish version.

How do you write a real estate sales script?

  1. Start from the buyer's questions, not the brochure: price, possession, loan, location, size, builder record.
  2. Write one clear answer to each, using only facts you can prove (approved plans, the RERA filing, the price sheet).
  3. Plan the questions you'll ask: configuration, budget, timeline, purpose, who else decides.
  4. End every conversation with a next step: a visit time, a document to send, a callback date.
  5. Practise out loud with a colleague playing the buyer, and update the script when a new objection keeps coming up.

What is a good real estate sales pitch? (Sample)

A good real estate sales pitch connects one buyer's need to one specific unit and ends with a clear next step. It takes about 30 seconds and follows four parts:

PartWhat it doesExample line
NeedRepeats what the buyer told you"You said you want a 3 BHK near the metro, under [budget], ready within two years."
MatchNames the unit that fits"The 3 BHK in Tower B fits that: [carpet area], east-facing, [real distance] from the metro."
ProofGives something they can check"Possession is [date] as filed with [state] RERA, and you can check the registration yourself."
Next stepAsks for one small action"Can I show it to you on Saturday at 11 or Sunday at 4?"

Full sample pitch (English):

"From what you've told me, you need a 3 BHK close to the metro, within [budget], with possession in about two years. The 3 BHK in Tower B fits all three: [carpet area] carpet, [real distance] to the station, and RERA possession in [month, year], which you can check on the RERA website. Photos only show so much. Would Saturday at 11 or Sunday at 4 suit you to see it?"

Hinglish sample: "Aapko metro ke paas 3 BHK chahiye tha, [budget] ke andar. Tower B ka 3 BHK bilkul fit hai: [carpet area], metro se [distance], aur RERA possession [date]. Ek baar dekh lijiye, Shanivaar 11 baje ya Ravivaar 4 baje?"

The pitch works because it is about the buyer, not the project. Leave out anything you can't prove: "best location", "prices will double" and "only a few units left" (unless true) weaken trust and can break the rules on misleading claims.

Script 1: calling a new enquiry

Call as soon as you can after the enquiry, ideally after an instant WhatsApp reply has gone out (how to automate that).

"Hi, am I speaking with [Name]? This is [your name] from [Project / company]. You enquired about our project in [locality] a little while ago. Is this a good time for two minutes?

Great. So I can tell you what's relevant, are you looking at a 2 BHK or a 3 BHK? … And roughly what budget? … Is this for your family to live in, or as an investment? … And when are you hoping to buy?

Based on that, we have [two options] that fit. The best way to judge them is to see the sample flat and the site. Would Saturday at 11 or Sunday at 4 suit you better?"

Hinglish opening: "Namaste [Name] ji, main [your name] bol raha hoon [Project] se. Aapne [locality] wale project ke baare mein enquiry ki thi. Do minute baat ho sakti hai?"

If they're busy: "No problem. Shall I WhatsApp you the brochure and call tomorrow at a time that suits you?" For the full list of qualifying questions, see real estate lead qualification questions.

Script 2: first WhatsApp reply and follow-ups

Hi [Name], thanks for your interest in [Project], [locality]. Here's the brochure and price list. Are you looking at 2 BHK or 3 BHK, and roughly what budget? I'll share the options that fit.

Follow-up after a day with no reply (add something new each time):

Hi [Name], sharing the floor plan of the 3 BHK on the higher floors. It has the park-facing balcony. Happy to send the cost sheet too. Would you like to see it in person this weekend?

On the WhatsApp Business Platform, messages sent outside the customer service window that opens when the buyer last messaged you must use approved templates (WhatsApp Business Platform docs). More wording in our real estate WhatsApp message templates.

Script 3: inviting and confirming a site visit

"From what you've told me, the [unit type] in Tower [B] fits best. Photos only show so much. You'll get a much better sense of the size and the view on site, and I can take you through the payment plan there. Saturday 11 or Sunday 4?"

Then confirm in writing with the date, time, Google Maps pin and the name and number of the person meeting them. More in how to book real estate site visits.

Script 4: handling common objections

ObjectionHow to respond
"The price is too high.""I understand. What are you comparing it with? … Let's put total cost and carpet area side by side. The difference often comes from what's included. If it still doesn't fit, I'll tell you honestly."
"Possession is too far away.""Fair concern. The RERA-registered possession date is [date]; you can check it on the [state] RERA website, and I'll show you this month's progress on site."
"The location is too far.""How do you usually commute? … From here it's [real distance/time] to [landmark] at peak hours. A weekday morning visit will show you the real drive."
"I'll think about it.""Of course. What's the main thing you're still unsure about? … I'll send exactly that and call you on [day]. Okay?"
"I need to discuss with family.""That makes sense. Would it help if they came for the visit too? Sunday is usually easier for families."
"Is the builder reliable?""Here are the projects they've delivered, with possession dates. You can see each one on the RERA website, and I can arrange a visit to a completed project."
"I'll wait for prices to fall.""That's your call, and I can't predict prices. What I can do is show you today's cost sheet and payment plan so you can compare when you're ready."

Never answer an objection with a promise you can't keep: no guaranteed returns, no invented possession dates, no amenities missing from the approved plans. Under Section 12 of the RERA Act, 2016, false statements in advertisements can make the promoter liable to compensate buyers, and Section 10 bars agents from unfair trade practices. See RERA advertisement rules.

Script 5: following up after a visit and asking for the booking

Same evening:

Hi [Name], thank you for visiting [Project] today. As promised, here's the cost sheet for [unit]. You'd asked about [concern]; I'll send the details tomorrow morning.

Asking for the booking, once the buyer's questions are answered:

"You've seen the unit, the cost sheet works, and your family liked the layout. Is there anything else you need to know before you decide? … Then shall I block [unit] for you and share the booking form?"

If they hesitate, ask what's holding them back and answer that one thing. Don't invent deadlines or discounts to force a decision.

Script 6: pitching to a property seller (resale listing)

Brokers also pitch to owners who want to sell or rent. Keep it about how you'll find the right buyer:

"Thanks for considering us for your flat in [society]. Here's how I'd sell it: photos and a short video that show the real layout, the listing on the portals buyers in this area use, and every enquiry answered on WhatsApp within minutes. I'll only bring buyers whose budget I've checked, and I'll update you every week with enquiries and visits. Shall we agree the asking price and the commission in writing?"

Agree commission terms in writing before you start; our guide to real estate broker commission in India covers how it usually works.

Script 7: cold calling, and the rules to follow

Calls to people who never enquired are regulated in India. Under TRAI's Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations, as amended in February 2025, ordinary 10-digit mobile numbers can't be used for telemarketing: promotional calls must come from registered senders using the 140 number series, customers can block them through the Do Not Disturb (DND) registry, and repeat violators can have their telecom connections disconnected (Storyboard18 report on the amendment; TRAI). India's DPDP Act, 2023 also requires a lawful basis, usually consent, to use personal data. Never use bought lists.

Where contact is legitimate, for example past enquirers who agreed to hear from you, keep it short and honest:

"Hi [Name], this is [your name] from [company]. You'd asked about homes in [locality] a few months ago. We've just opened a new tower in [project]. Is that still something you're looking at? … No problem at all. Thanks for your time."

How do you train a sales team on scripts?

  • Role-play the top objections every week, with one person as the buyer.
  • Listen to real calls (with the buyer's consent where calls are recorded) and update the script with what worked.
  • Track results by salesperson: calls to visits booked, visits to bookings.
  • Keep a shared fact sheet per project: prices, sizes, distances, possession date, RERA number. No guessing on calls.
