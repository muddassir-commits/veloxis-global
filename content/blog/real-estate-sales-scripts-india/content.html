A real estate sales script is a planned outline of what to say at each stage, from the first call and WhatsApp reply to the site visit invitation, objections and follow-up, so every salesperson gives buyers clear, consistent answers. A good script isn't read word for word. It's a structure: open, ask, listen, match, invite. The goal of the first call is almost never to sell the home. It's to understand the buyer and book a site visit.

Below are real estate sales pitch examples and scripts for calling a new lead, the first WhatsApp reply, inviting a site visit, handling the most common objections, following up, and calling cold numbers. Each comes in English with a Hinglish version where it helps.

How to write a real estate sales script

  1. Start from the buyer's questions, not the brochure. List what buyers ask most: price, possession, loan, location, size, builder record.
  2. Write one clear answer to each, using only facts you can prove (the approved plans, the RERA filing, the price sheet).
  3. Plan the questions you'll ask: configuration, budget, timeline, purpose (living or investment), who else decides.
  4. End every conversation with a next step: a site visit time, a document to send, a callback date.
  5. Practise it out loud with a colleague playing the buyer, and update the script whenever a new objection keeps coming up.

Script 1: calling a new enquiry

Call as soon as you can after the enquiry, ideally after an instant WhatsApp reply has already gone out (how to automate that).

"Hi, am I speaking with [Name]? This is [your name] from [Project / company]. You enquired about our project in [locality] a little while ago. Is this a good time for two minutes?

Great. So I can tell you what's actually relevant, are you looking at a 2 BHK or a 3 BHK? … And roughly what budget are you working with? … Is this for your family to live in, or as an investment? … And when are you hoping to buy?

Based on that, we have [two options] that fit. The best way to judge them is to see the sample flat and the site. Would Saturday at 11 or Sunday at 4 suit you better?"

Hinglish opening: "Namaste [Name] ji, main [your name] bol raha hoon [Project] se. Aapne [locality] wale project ke baare mein enquiry ki thi. Do minute baat ho sakti hai?"

If they're busy: "No problem. Shall I WhatsApp you the brochure and call tomorrow at a time that suits you?"

Script 2: first WhatsApp reply and follow-ups

Hi [Name], thanks for your interest in [Project], [locality]. Here's the brochure and price list. Are you looking at 2 BHK or 3 BHK, and roughly what budget? I'll share the options that fit.

Follow-up after one day with no reply (add something new each time):

Hi [Name], sharing the floor plan of the 3 BHK on the higher floors. It has the park-facing balcony. Happy to send the cost sheet too. Would you like to see it in person this weekend?

Script 3: inviting and confirming a site visit

"From what you've told me, the [unit type] in Tower [B] fits best. Photos only show so much. You'll get a much better sense of the size and the view on site, and I can take you through the payment plan there. Saturday 11 or Sunday 4?"

Then confirm in writing with the date, time, Google Maps pin and the name and number of the person meeting them. More in how to book real estate site visits.

Script 4: handling common objections

ObjectionHow to respond
"The price is too high.""I understand. Can I ask what you're comparing it with? … Let's look at the total cost and carpet area side by side. The difference often comes from what's included. If it still doesn't fit, I'll tell you honestly."
"Possession is too far away.""That's a fair concern. The RERA-registered possession date is [date]. You can check it yourself on the [state] RERA website, and I'll show you this month's construction progress on site."
"The location is too far.""How do you usually commute? … From here it's [real distance/time] to [landmark] at peak hours. Visiting on a weekday morning will show you the real drive."
"I'll think about it.""Of course. What's the main thing you're still unsure about? … I'll send you exactly that, and call you on [day]. Is that okay?"
"I need to discuss with family.""That makes sense. Would it help if they came for the visit too? Sunday is usually easier for families."
"Is the builder reliable?""Here are the projects they've delivered, with possession dates. You can see each one on the RERA website, and I can arrange a visit to a completed project."

Never answer an objection with a promise you can't keep: no guaranteed returns, no invented possession dates, no amenities that aren't in the approved plans. Under Section 12 of the RERA Act, false statements in advertisements and prospectuses can make the promoter liable to compensate buyers, and agents must not use unfair trade practices under Section 10. See RERA advertisement rules.

Script 5: cold calling, and the rules to follow

Calls to people who never enquired convert far less often than calls to enquiries, and they're regulated. TRAI's rules on unsolicited commercial communication restrict promotional calls and messages to numbers registered on the Do Not Disturb (DND) registry. Check the current requirements on the TRAI website before running any calling campaign, and never use bought lists. India's DPDP Act, 2023 also requires a lawful basis, usually consent, to use personal data.

Where calling is permitted, for example to past enquirers, existing customers or businesses you work with, keep it short and honest:

"Hi [Name], this is [your name] from [company]. You'd asked about homes in [locality] a few months ago. We've just opened a new tower in [project]. Is that still something you're looking at? … No problem at all. Thanks for your time."

How to train a team on scripts

  • Role-play the top objections every week, with one person as the buyer.
  • Listen to real calls (with the buyer's consent where call recording is used) and update the script with what worked.
  • Track results by salesperson: calls to visits booked, visits to bookings.
  • Keep a shared fact sheet for each project: prices, sizes, distances, possession date, RERA number. No guessing on calls.
