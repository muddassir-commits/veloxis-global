Notice the psychological difference between amateur pitching and authoritative consulting:

✕ The Amateur Opener (Fails 85% of the time):

"Namaste sir, main XYZ Developers se baat kar raha hoon. Hamara Sector 150 mein luxury project launch hua hai, kya aap 3 BHK mein interested hain?"

✓ The Veloxis Consultative Opener (68% Conversion Rate):

Mastering the Top 3 Indian Real Estate Objections

1. "Sir, aapka rate market se kaafi zyaada hai"

Wrong response: "Nahi sir, hamara construction quality best hai."
Winning response: "Bilkul sahi sawaal hai, Sharma ji. Agar sirf super area rate dekhein toh aisa lag sakta hai. Par hamara carpet area efficiency 78% hai jabki nearby projects mein sirf 65% carpet milta hai. Yani effectively aap carpet area par ₹400/sq.ft. bacha rahe hain. Saturday subah sample flat inspection mein 15 minutes nikal payenge?"

2. "Location kaafi door lag rahi hai"

Winning response: "Understandable sir. 3 saal pehle Sector 137 bhi door lagta tha. Par next 8 months mein naya metro extension aur express highway link operational ho raha hai, jisse drive time sirf 12 minutes reh jayega. Is price advantage ka benefit lene ke liye early launch window sabse critical hai."

The Non-Negotiable Rule of Site Visit Closing
