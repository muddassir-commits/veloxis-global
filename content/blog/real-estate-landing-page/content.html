A real estate landing page for an Indian project needs eight blocks in this order: a first screen with locality, configurations, starting price and RERA number; the RERA block with QR code; location; floor plans in carpet area; price sheet on request; proof; a short form with consent text; and a WhatsApp button. Everything else is optional.

This is a build template for developers, brokers and channel partners who send Google or Meta ad clicks to a project page. Most guides that rank for this topic show US agent pages: home valuations, open houses, testimonials. They skip what Indian buyers and regulators expect: the RERA number, state QR codes, carpet area, consent wording under the DPDP Act, WhatsApp, Hindi and English, and phones on mobile data. If you want the reasoning behind each fix rather than the blueprint, read our real estate landing page best practices first.

What sections should a real estate landing page have?

Eight sections, ordered by the questions a buyer asks: where is it, what can I buy, is it legal, what does it cost, can I trust you, and how do I ask. Use the table as your wireframe. The "copy prompt" column is the sentence you fill in with your project's real, approved facts.

What should the first screen say?

The first phone screen should let a buyer decide in seconds whether the project fits them: locality, configurations, starting price, RERA number and one clear button. A render with a slogan answers none of these.

Match the headline to the ad. If the Google ad says "Ready-to-move 3 BHK in Sector [x]", the page should open with the same words. Google calls this landing page experience, which it describes as how relevant and useful your page is to people who click your ad, and it is one part of Quality Score (Google Ads Help). For big projects, build one page per configuration or buyer type instead of one page for everything.

Label renders as an artist's impression, and use the button text to say what happens next: "Get price sheet on WhatsApp" is clearer than "Submit".

How do you show the RERA number and QR code?

Put the RERA registration number on the first screen and repeat it, with the state authority's website and any required QR code, in its own block near the top. A landing page that promotes a project is an advertisement, and Section 11(2) of the RERA Act, 2016 requires a promoter's advertisements to mention the authority's website address and the registration number prominently.

Some states ask for more. MahaRERA made a QR code mandatory on project advertisements from 1 August 2023 (Housing.com), and UP RERA has asked for QR codes on project brochures and ads (PTI via The Week). If a channel partner runs the page, add their own RERA agent registration number too. Our guides to RERA advertisement rules and the RERA number check cover the state details.

Show areas in carpet area, the measure the Act defines in Section 2(k), and keep every number on the page the same as the brochure, the ad and the agreement.

Should the page show the price or a price sheet on request?

Show a starting price or band on the page, and offer the full cost sheet on request. The starting price filters out buyers who can't afford the project, so your team calls fewer people who were never going to buy. The cost sheet, which lists every charge, is the natural reason for the buyer to share their number.

  • Take the starting price from your current approved price list, and update the page the day the price list changes.
  • Say what the starting price includes. Use "all-inclusive" only if it truly is.
  • Show the payment plan stages (booking, construction-linked, possession) without promising dates you can't keep.
  • Never promise returns, rental yield or appreciation. They don't belong in an advertisement.

What should the enquiry form ask, and what consent text does it need?

Ask for name, mobile number, configuration and budget, and add one plain line saying how you will use the number. Use tap buttons for configuration and budget instead of typed fields, and pre-select "+91" for the mobile number.

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 expects personal data to be collected with notice and used for the purpose it was given for (MeitY). The DPDP Rules, 2025 were notified in November 2025 with an eighteen-month phased compliance period (PIB), so build the notice into the form now rather than later. A simple pattern:

"We will use your number to send the price sheet and details of [project] by call, SMS and WhatsApp. You can ask us to stop at any time. Privacy policy."
  • Link the privacy policy and name the business that will contact the buyer.
  • If channel partners or a call centre will see the lead, say so in the policy.
  • Record the source, date and consent text against each lead in your real estate CRM.

How do you add a WhatsApp button that tracks the source?

Use WhatsApp's click-to-chat link with a pre-filled message that names the project and a short source code, so every chat tells you which page and ad it came from. WhatsApp's format is https://wa.me/<number>?text=<message>, with the number in full international format without "+", zeros, brackets or dashes, and the message URL-encoded (WhatsApp Help Center).

Where the button sitsPre-filled message (before encoding)

Mark the WhatsApp click as a conversion in Google Ads and Meta, and tag each campaign link with UTM parameters so the visit is credited in GA4 (Google Analytics Help). Then reply in minutes, not hours: our guide to WhatsApp automation for real estate leads shows how.

Should the page be in Hindi, English or both?

Use the language of the ad. If you run Hindi and English ads, build two pages, one per language, and link them to each other. Google recommends a separate URL for each language version, hreflang annotations to connect them, and keeping each page in one language for both content and navigation. It advises against redirecting visitors automatically by their assumed language (Google Search Central).

  • Put a visible "हिंदी / English" switch at the top of both pages.
  • Keep the RERA number, carpet areas and prices identical in both versions.
  • Have a native speaker write the Hindi copy; machine translation of property terms reads badly.
  • Send Hindi ads to the Hindi page, so the ad and page still match.

How do you make the page fast on budget phones?

Make the hero image small and load it first, load everything else later, and test on a throttled mid-range phone profile, not your office Wi-Fi. Google's target is a Largest Contentful Paint of 2.5 seconds or less, an Interaction to Next Paint of 200 milliseconds or less and a Cumulative Layout Shift of 0.1 or less, measured at the 75th percentile of visits (web.dev).

  • Hero image: compress it, serve WebP or AVIF, and size it for phones. Never lazy-load it, and give it fetchpriority="high" (web.dev: Optimize LCP).
  • Everything below: lazy-load gallery images and floor plans (web.dev: lazy loading).
  • No sliders or autoplay video on the first screen. Link to the walkthrough video instead.
  • Scripts: load chat widgets, heatmaps and extra pixels after the page is usable; keep only the tags you use.
  • Test: run PageSpeed Insights on the live URL, and use Chrome DevTools network and CPU throttling to see the page as a buyer on a budget phone does.

How do you build and launch the page, step by step?

Collect approved facts first, then write, build, test and connect tracking before any ad goes live.

  1. Fact sheet: get the RERA number, QR code, carpet areas, current price list, payment plan and approved amenity list in writing from the developer.
  2. One page per promise: decide which configurations, buyer types and languages each need their own page.
  3. Write the copy using the table above, with every claim traceable to the fact sheet.
  4. Build the page with no menu, a sticky WhatsApp and Call bar, and the form with consent text.
  5. Set up tracking: form submits, WhatsApp clicks and calls as conversions, UTM links per campaign and channel partner, and the source saved in the CRM.
  6. Test on real phones: submit the form, tap WhatsApp, scan the QR code, and check PageSpeed Insights.
  7. Compliance check: RERA number, website, QR code, disclaimers and prices match the brochure and the ads.
  8. Launch and review weekly: compare enquiries and site visits by ad, and fix the section where buyers drop off.

The page is one part of the funnel. Ads feed it (see Google Ads for real estate in India), and the follow-up decides whether an enquiry becomes a site visit (see how to book real estate site visits).
