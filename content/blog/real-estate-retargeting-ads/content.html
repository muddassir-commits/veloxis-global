Real estate retargeting ads show your project again to people who already showed interest (they visited your landing page, watched your video, opened your lead form or messaged you) but did not book a site visit. To retarget website visitors, install the Meta Pixel and Google tag, build audiences by how close each person came to enquiring, and show each group a new ad that answers what stopped them.

Buying a home takes weeks or months, so most interested people won't act on the first ad. This guide explains how retargeting works on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google, a step-by-step way to retarget website visitors, which audiences to build for a real estate project, what to show each one, budget and frequency, and the privacy rules to follow in India.

How does retargeting work in real estate?

Retargeting (also called remarketing) needs two things: a way to recognise people who interacted with you, and an ad platform that can show ads to that group.

  • On your website or landing page: the Meta Pixel and the Google tag record visits and actions, such as viewing the floor plan page or starting a form. Meta's Conversions API can send the same events from your server, which helps when browsers block tracking.
  • On the platforms themselves: Meta can group people who engaged with your Facebook Page, Instagram account, videos or lead forms, without them ever visiting your site. Google can do the same for people who watched your YouTube videos.
  • From your own list: you can upload a customer list (such as past enquiries) and the platform matches it to its users, if you have consent to use the data this way.

Each group becomes an audience with its own ads and budget. Without the Pixel or tag on your landing page, the most valuable audiences stay empty, so start there. Our landing page guide covers what the page itself needs.

How do you retarget website visitors in real estate with ads?

  1. Tag the site. Install the Meta Pixel and the Google tag on every project page, and track key events: floor plan view, price page view, form start, form submit, WhatsApp click.
  2. Separate pages by project. Each project needs its own URL, so you can build an audience of people who saw that project only.
  3. Build audiences on Meta (Website Custom Audiences) and Google (data segments) from those pages and events.
  4. Exclude people who already submitted a form or booked, using your CRM list or the form-submit event.
  5. Write one ad per audience that answers the question they likely had (see the table below).
  6. Send clicks back to the same project page or straight to WhatsApp, never the homepage.
  7. Measure site visits from retargeted leads in your CRM, and refresh creatives when frequency climbs and results fall.

Which retargeting audiences should a real estate project build?

Build audiences by how close each person came to enquiring. The closer they came, the more specific your ad can be.

Always exclude people who already booked, and usually people who enquired in the last few days, because your sales team is already talking to them. Showing "Enquire now" ads to someone who booked last week wastes money and looks careless.

How do you set up retargeting on Meta (Facebook and Instagram)?

  1. Install the Meta Pixel on your landing page and set up the events you care about, ideally with the Conversions API as well.
  2. Create Custom Audiences in Ads Manager: Website (visitors to specific pages), Video (viewers of your project videos), Instagram account and Facebook Page (engagers), and Lead form (people who opened or submitted). Meta's website custom audiences documentation lists a retention window of 1 to 180 days for website audiences; check the current limit in Ads Manager.
  3. Pick the window to match the buying cycle. A launch might use the last few weeks; a ready-to-move project with slower decisions can use longer windows.
  4. Create a separate ad set for each audience so each gets its own message and you can see what works.
  5. Answer the Special Ad Category question correctly. According to Meta's documentation, the housing category must be declared when ads reach the US, Canada or Europe (relevant for NRI retargeting); for ads shown only in India it is optional. When it is on, lookalike audiences are unavailable and some targeting is limited.

How do you set up retargeting on Google?

  • Install the Google tag (through Google Tag Manager or directly) on your landing page and link Google Analytics to Google Ads.
  • Create data segments (the new name for remarketing lists) from website visitors, YouTube viewers or customer lists. Google's data segments help page gives a default membership duration of 30 days and a maximum of 540 days; pick a window that suits your project.
  • Use them in Display, YouTube, Demand Gen and Performance Max campaigns, or as an "observation" signal on Search campaigns, so you can bid more for returning visitors who search for your project or area again.
  • Update your privacy policy. Google's personalised advertising policy sets rules for remarketing, and sites using it should tell visitors.

What should real estate retargeting ads show?

Something new that answers the question that stopped the person last time. Don't repeat the ad they already ignored.

How much budget and how long should real estate retargeting run?

Retargeting audiences are small compared with cold audiences, so they usually need only a modest part of the ad budget. Watch three things:

  • Frequency: how many times, on average, each person has seen your ads. If it climbs and results fall, people are tired of the ads; change the creative or widen the window.
  • Audience size: very small audiences may not deliver at all. Combine an audience that is too small with a similar one (all landing page visitors instead of only the price page).
  • Cost per site visit, not just cost per lead: retargeted leads are often warmer. Judge them by how many visit, as explained in our real estate marketing budget guide.

Stop retargeting once someone books, asks you to stop, or has been in the audience long past your buying cycle.

What privacy rules apply to retargeting in India?

Retargeting uses personal data, so do it openly:

  • Tell visitors. Your privacy policy should say you use cookies and pixels for advertising, including remarketing.
  • Get consent for customer lists. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 expects personal data to be used for the purpose people agreed to. Only upload lists of people who agreed to hear from you, and check the current rules with your lawyer.
  • Don't target by sensitive traits. Never include or exclude people based on religion, caste or similar characteristics. It is wrong, and the platforms forbid it.
  • Respect "no". Remove anyone who asks you to stop from your uploaded lists.

What are common real estate retargeting mistakes?

  • No tracking on the landing page, so the most valuable audiences never fill up.
  • The same ad to everyone, cold and warm, instead of a message for each stage.
  • Not excluding buyers and recent leads.
  • Sending retargeted clicks to the homepage instead of the project page they already saw.
  • Letting ads run for months unchanged until people stop noticing them.
  • Treating retargeting as a replacement for follow-up. A lead who submitted a form needs a call and a WhatsApp message first. Our lead qualification guide shows what to ask, and a real estate CRM keeps track of who still needs a visit.
