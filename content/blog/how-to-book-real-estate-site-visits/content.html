To book more site visits from online enquiries: reply within minutes, ask two or three qualifying questions on WhatsApp, offer two specific visit times instead of asking "when can you come?", send the location pin and a reminder, and follow up anyone who doesn't show. A site visit is where most property decisions get made, so every step between the enquiry and the visit should be designed to get the buyer to the site.

This guide covers what a site visit is, a step-by-step booking process, what to say on WhatsApp and on the call, a site visit form and report you can copy, how to reduce no-shows, and how to measure your visit rate.

What is a site visit in real estate?

A site visit is when a prospective buyer visits the project (the sample flat, the site office or the actual tower) with a salesperson or channel partner. For under-construction projects it is often the buyer's first real look at the location, the quality of construction and the people behind it. Few buyers book a home they haven't seen, so for most builders and brokers the site visit is the step that matters most after the enquiry.

How to book a site visit: step by step

  1. Reply within minutes. Send an instant WhatsApp message with the brochure or a short summary, the price band and a question. A study in Harvard Business Review found that firms contacting online leads within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to qualify them as firms that waited longer.
  2. Qualify in two or three questions. Which configuration? What budget range? Buying in the next three months or later?
  3. Call with context. The salesperson calls knowing the answers and talks about the buyer's needs, not a script about the project.
  4. Offer two specific times. "Saturday 11 am or Sunday 4 pm?" gets a decision more easily than an open question.
  5. Confirm in writing. Send the date, time, Google Maps pin, the name and number of the person who will meet them, and what to bring.
  6. Remind. A reminder the day before and a short message on the morning of the visit.
  7. Follow up after. Thank them the same day, answer what they asked, and set the next step.

Steps 1, 5 and 6 can be automated. Steps 2 to 4 work best with a person. See WhatsApp automation for real estate leads.

What to say: WhatsApp and call scripts

First WhatsApp reply:

Hi [Name], thanks for your interest in [Project], [locality]. Here's the brochure and price list. Quick question so I can share the right options: are you looking at 2 BHK or 3 BHK, and roughly what budget?

On the call:

"I saw you're looking at a 3 BHK under [budget]. We have two that fit, one on a higher floor with a park view. The best way to judge is to see the sample flat and the site. Would Saturday at 11 or Sunday at 4 work better for you?"

Confirmation:

Confirmed: Saturday 11 am at [Project] site office. Location: [Google Maps link]. [Salesperson name] will meet you, [phone]. Parking is available at the gate.

Real estate site visit form (template)

Use a site visit form, on paper, a tablet or a Google Form, so every visit is recorded the same way and nothing is lost when the buyer leaves. Fields to include:

SectionFields
VisitorName, mobile, email, current city or locality, number of people visiting
SourceHow they heard (Meta ad, Google, portal, CP name and RERA number, referral, hoarding, walk-in)
RequirementConfiguration, budget range, purpose (living or investment), timeline, home loan needed
VisitDate, time, salesperson, units or sample flat shown
FeedbackWhat they liked, objections, other projects they're considering
ConsentPermission to contact them by phone and WhatsApp about this project
Next stepSecond visit, family visit, EOI, booking or closed (with reason), and the date

The source field matters most for marketing: it is how you learn which ads and channel partners actually bring visitors. The consent field reflects India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, which expects personal data to be used for the purpose it was given for.

Site visit report example

A short report after each visit keeps the next conversation specific. For example:

Visit: Sat 11 am. Rahul and Priya S., currently renting in Indirapuram. 3 BHK, budget up to ₹1.1 crore, buying within 3 months, home loan pre-approved. Saw Tower B sample flat and site. Liked: layout, park-facing units. Concern: possession date and distance to school. Also considering [competitor]. Next: send construction update video and school distances on Monday; invite parents for a second visit Sunday.

(Names and figures above are an illustration, not a real buyer.)

How to reduce site visit no-shows

  • Book only qualified leads. A visit booked with someone whose budget doesn't fit is likely to be skipped.
  • Get a specific time, not "this weekend".
  • Remind twice: the evening before and the morning of the visit, with the location pin.
  • Make rescheduling easy: "Reply 1 to confirm, 2 to change the time." A rescheduled visit is better than a no-show.
  • Call no-shows the same day, without pressure, and offer the next two slots.
  • Offer help with travel where it fits, such as directions from the nearest metro station or a pickup for families.

What is a good site visit conversion rate?

We haven't found a reliable public benchmark for India. Rates depend heavily on the source of the lead, the price segment, the location and how fast the team replies, so a figure quoted without its source tells you little. Measure your own instead:

  • Lead to visit booked: visits booked ÷ leads, by source.
  • Visit show rate: visits done ÷ visits booked.
  • Visit to booking: bookings ÷ visits done.
  • Cost per site visit: ad spend ÷ visits done, by campaign.
