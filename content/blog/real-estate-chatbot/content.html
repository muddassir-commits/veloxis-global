A real estate chatbot should do four jobs well: reply to every enquiry in seconds, ask the few questions that qualify a buyer, share approved project facts and book the site visit, then hand the conversation to a person. It should never quote prices it can't confirm, promise possession dates, or pretend to be human.

This guide is for builders, brokers and channel partners in India who are thinking about a chatbot on WhatsApp or on their website. It covers what the bot should and shouldn't do, a ready conversation script, when to hand over to a salesperson, what WhatsApp's business rules and RERA mean for bot replies, and how to set one up without annoying buyers. We don't rank vendors: most "best chatbot" lists are written by chatbot companies.

What is a real estate chatbot?

A real estate chatbot is software that answers property enquiries automatically, on WhatsApp, a website chat window or Instagram, and passes qualified buyers to a salesperson. Some bots follow fixed buttons and menus; others use AI to understand free-typed questions. Most good set-ups mix both.

Think of it as the front desk of your sales office that never closes. It greets the buyer, collects the basics, shares the brochure and floor plans you have approved, and books a visit slot. It is not the salesperson. Buyers making one of the biggest purchases of their life still want to talk to someone who knows the project.

What should a real estate chatbot actually do?

The bot should handle the repetitive first five minutes of every enquiry, so your team spends its time on buyers who are ready to talk. These are the jobs worth automating:

  • Instant first reply. A lead from a Meta ad, Google ad or portal gets a greeting within seconds, day or night. Research published in Harvard Business Review found most companies do not respond to online leads nearly fast enough; a bot fixes the first reply, even if your team calls back later.
  • Qualification. Budget, configuration (1, 2 or 3 BHK, plot size), preferred location, timeline, and whether the buyer needs a home loan. Keep it to the questions in our lead qualification guide; four or five is plenty.
  • Approved project information. Brochure PDF, floor plans, location map, amenities list, RERA registration number and the regulator's website. Only content your sales head has signed off.
  • Site visit booking. Offer two or three real slots, confirm, and send a reminder with the location pin. Our guide on booking real estate site visits covers the follow-up.
  • CRM entry. Every answer goes into the lead record with the source and campaign, so the caller sees it before dialling. See our real estate CRM guide for the fields.
  • Hand-off. A clear button or keyword for "talk to a person", plus automatic hand-off when the bot is out of its depth.

What should a real estate chatbot never do?

A bot should never say anything your company would not put in a signed advertisement, because under RERA what you tell buyers can bind you. These are the lines to draw before you write a single reply:

Never let the botWhyWhat it should say instead
Quote a final price or "today only" offerPrices change by floor, view and payment plan; a wrong quote creates a dispute"Prices depend on the unit. Our sales advisor will share the current price sheet."
Promise a possession dateThe binding date is the one in the agreement and on the RERA registration"The RERA-registered completion date is on the project page at the regulator's website."
Guarantee returns, rental yield or appreciationIt is a promise you cannot keep, and false claims in advertisements carry liability under the RERA ActSay nothing about returns.
Advertise a project that isn't RERA-registeredSection 3 of the Act bars advertising unregistered projects that need registrationCollect interest only after registration, if your lawyer agrees.
Pretend to be a personBuyers feel cheated when they find out, and trust is the whole sale"I'm the project's virtual assistant. A person from our team can take over any time."
Ask for Aadhaar, PAN or bank details in chatSensitive data needs a secure process and a clear purpose under the DPDP ActCollect documents at the booking stage through a secure channel.

What does a good chatbot conversation look like?

A good chatbot conversation is short: greet, ask four or five questions with buttons, share one useful thing, offer a visit, and hand over. Here is a script you can adapt for a residential project. Words in square brackets are yours to fill in.

  1. Greeting and disclosure. "Hi, thanks for your interest in [Project], [City]. I'm the project's virtual assistant. Project RERA no. [number], details at [regulator website]. Can I ask a few quick questions so we send you the right options?" Buttons: Yes / Talk to a person.
  2. Configuration. "Which home are you looking for?" Buttons: 2 BHK / 3 BHK / 4 BHK / Not sure yet.
  3. Budget. "Which budget range suits you?" Offer three or four ranges that match your actual inventory, plus "Prefer not to say".
  4. Timeline. "When are you planning to buy?" Buttons: Within 3 months / 3 to 6 months / Just exploring.
  5. Purpose and loan. "Is this for living or investment, and will you need a home loan?" Buttons for each.
  6. Share and offer a visit. Send the approved brochure and floor plan for their configuration, then: "Would you like to visit the site or the sample flat?" Show two or three real slots.
  7. Confirm and hand over. "Booked: [day, time]. [Advisor's first name] from our team will call you to confirm. Reply STOP any time to stop messages."

Every reply must also accept free text. If a buyer types "what is the carpet area of 3 BHK", the bot either answers from the approved fact sheet or hands over; it never guesses.

Once the visit is booked, the follow-up messages come from approved WhatsApp templates. Our real estate WhatsApp message templates include reminder, missed-visit and post-visit messages.

When should the chatbot hand over to a human?

Hand over the moment a buyer asks for a person, shows strong intent, or asks something the bot can't answer from approved facts. WhatsApp's own Business Messaging Policy says businesses that use automation must have "prompt, clear, and direct escalation paths", such as an in-chat transfer to a human agent, a phone number, an email or a support form.

Set these hand-off triggers:

  • The buyer taps "Talk to a person" or types words like call, agent, human or speak.
  • The buyer chose "Within 3 months" and a budget that matches available stock (a hot lead).
  • Questions about price negotiation, payment plans, loan approval, legal title or possession.
  • A complaint, an existing booking, or an NRI buyer asking about documents.
  • The bot fails to understand the buyer twice in a row.

The hand-off is only as good as the response behind it. Decide who picks up, how quickly during working hours, and what the bot says outside them ("Our team works 10 am to 7 pm. [Name] will call you first thing tomorrow."). Then make sure the person sees the whole chat history in the CRM, so the buyer never repeats themselves.

What do WhatsApp's rules mean for a property chatbot?

On WhatsApp, a property chatbot must run on the official WhatsApp Business Platform (the API), message only people who opted in, reply freely only within 24 hours of the buyer's last message, and use approved templates after that. Do RERA and the DPDP Act apply to chatbot replies?

Yes. A chatbot reply that promotes a project is part of your advertising, and the buyer data it collects is personal data. Plan for both from day one.

RERA. Section 11(2) of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 requires every advertisement to show the regulator's website address and the project's registration number. Some states go further: in April 2025 MahaRERA required the registration number, QR code and website to be displayed prominently in all project advertisements, and the circular names WhatsApp and other messaging services, as reported by the Free Press Journal. Put the registration details in the bot's first message and in the brochure it shares.

DPDP. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 requires a lawful basis, such as consent, to process personal data. Tell buyers what you collect and why, store only what sales needs, and delete or stop messaging when they ask.

This is general information, not legal advice. Rules were checked on 5 November 2026; confirm your state's RERA requirements with your lawyer.

How do you set up a real estate chatbot step by step?

Start with one project, one channel and one simple flow, then improve it from real conversations.

  1. Write the fact sheet. Configurations, carpet areas, amenities, location, RERA number, approved brochure and plans. The bot answers only from this.
  2. Choose the channel. WhatsApp through an official Business Solution Provider for Indian buyers; a website chat widget that can continue the conversation on WhatsApp.
  3. Build the script above with buttons, plus a fallback and hand-off for anything else.
  4. Connect the CRM so every answer, source and campaign lands on the lead record and creates a call task.
  5. Get templates approved for reminders and follow-ups outside the 24-hour window.
  6. Test with your own team on real phones: wrong spellings, Hindi or Hinglish replies, voice notes and "call me".
  7. Review chats every week. Read transcripts where buyers dropped off or asked for a person, and fix the step that failed.

Track a few simple measures: share of enquiries that get a reply, share that complete qualification, site visits booked, and hand-offs answered on time. Compare them by campaign in your CRM, not by gut feel.

Website chatbot or WhatsApp chatbot: which is better?

For most Indian property buyers, WhatsApp is the better home for the main conversation, and the website chat should hand over to it. A website chat disappears when the buyer closes the tab; a WhatsApp thread stays on their phone, with your brochure and visit details in one place.
