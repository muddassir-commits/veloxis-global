To generate real estate leads in India, combine free sources (Google Business Profile, referrals, portal listings, walkthrough videos, WhatsApp updates to past enquirers and channel partner tie-ups) with paid sources (Google search ads, Meta ads sent to a project landing page, and paid portal plans). Then judge every source on cost per site visit, not number of enquiries.

Ask builders and brokers about leads and you rarely hear "we get too few". You hear "the leads are junk", "the numbers don't pick up" or "lots of leads, very few site visits". So this guide covers both halves: where real estate leads come from, free and paid, and how to make sure the ones you get turn into site visits.

What counts as a real estate lead?

A lead is anyone who left a name and number about a property you can sell, but only some become buyers. Most arguments between builders, CPs and agencies come from counting different things, so agree on these stages before you spend a rupee:

StageWhat it means
EnquirySomeone left a name and number: a form, call, WhatsApp message or portal lead.
ContactedYour team or chatbot actually spoke to them.
QualifiedBudget, configuration, location and timeline match something you can sell.
Site visitThey came to the site or sample flat. This is the number that predicts bookings.
BookingThey paid the booking amount.

Judge every lead source on cost per site visit, not cost per enquiry. A source with cheap enquiries and no visits is the most expensive one you have. Our list of lead qualification questions helps you decide who counts as qualified.

How to generate real estate leads for free in India

Free lead generation costs time instead of money. If you are a broker, channel partner or small developer starting out, work through these in order:

  1. Set up and complete your Google Business Profile. Google says local ranking depends on relevance, distance and prominence, and that complete, accurate information helps (Google Business Profile Help). Add your service area, categories, real photos, your WhatsApp number and regular posts. Our local SEO guide for agents walks through it.
  2. Ask for Google reviews the right way. Ask every buyer, seller and tenant you helped, with a direct link, as Google suggests in its tips to get more reviews. Never buy or fake reviews.
  3. List on property portals. Most portals let owners and agents post listings, with free and paid options. Use real photos, the exact locality and a clear price, and reply to every portal enquiry within minutes.
  4. Build a referral habit. Ask past buyers at booking, at possession and a few months after. A simple real estate referral program turns this into a system.
  5. Post short walkthrough and locality videos on Instagram and YouTube with your WhatsApp number on screen: sample flats, road access, nearby schools and metro. Our guide to real estate social media covers what to post.
  6. Work with developers as a registered channel partner. Agents must register with the state RERA under Section 9 of the RERA Act, 2016; see RERA registration for agents. Developers share launch material and sometimes leads with registered partners.
  7. Keep an opt-in WhatsApp list of past enquiries and send useful updates (new inventory, price changes, site events), not daily forwards.
  8. Reply fast to every enquiry you already get. The cheapest lead is the one you don't lose.

Free methods compound slowly. Expect weeks before a Business Profile or video channel brings steady enquiries, so start them early and keep at them.

How to generate leads for a real estate business with paid ads

Paid lead generation means your own Google and Meta ads, sent to a project landing page, plus paid portal plans. Your own ads produce leads nobody else receives, which is why they usually convert better than shared portal enquiries when handled well.

  • Google search ads catch people typing "3 BHK in Sector 150 Noida" or your project name. Fewer leads, usually closer to buying. Google also offers lead form assets that collect details inside the ad. See Google Ads for real estate.
  • Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram) reach people before they search: essential for launches, EOI drives and retargeting. See Meta ads for real estate in India.
  • Retargeting shows ads to people who visited your landing page but didn't enquire. See real estate retargeting ads.
  • Paid portal plans give listings more visibility; judge them on visits, like any other source.

If you can only fund one channel, read our Google Ads vs Meta Ads comparison, and plan spend with the real estate marketing budget guide. Whichever you use, send ads to a project landing page, not your homepage or a portal listing, and track every lead back to the ad that produced it.

A common question is whether Google runs a special lead generation program for real estate in India. Google doesn't sell leads; it offers ads (search, lead forms, Performance Max) and the free Business Profile. Leads come from how you use them.

Why do you get junk real estate leads, and how do you fix it?

Most "fake leads" are not fake. They are real people who were the wrong audience, clicked too easily, or were called too late. The usual causes and fixes:

ProblemFix
Meta instant form too easy to submitChoose the higher-intent form option with a review step, and add 2 or 3 qualifying questions (budget, configuration, timeline).
Wrong audience: people who can't afford itShow the price band in the ad and on the form. Buyers outside the budget filter themselves out.
Ads optimising for form fillsSend qualified leads and site visits back to Meta (Conversions API) and Google (offline conversion imports) so they learn what a buyer looks like.
Irrelevant Google searchesNegative keywords: rent, PG, jobs, resale (for new launches), free, course.
Numbers don't pick upReply on WhatsApp within seconds; many buyers won't answer an unknown number but will reply to a message with the brochure.
Called once, then forgottenA follow-up sequence: WhatsApp, call, reminder, update, spread over days, not one attempt.

How fast should you respond to a property lead?

Within minutes, ideally seconds. Property buyers often enquire with several projects in the same evening, and the first project to reply with the price sheet and a visit slot is well placed to get the visit. That is why the fastest improvement for most teams isn't more ad budget; it's an instant reply to every enquiry, day and night.

Should you buy real estate leads or databases in India?

Avoid bought phone number lists and "verified buyer databases". The reasons are practical and legal:

  • The people on those lists never asked to hear from you, so contact rates are low and complaints are common.
  • TRAI's February 2025 amendments to its commercial communication rules make spam complaints easier, allow complaints within 7 days instead of 3, and speed up action against senders, including disconnection and blacklisting for repeat violations (TRAI press release 11/2025).
  • The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules notified on 14 November 2025 require a clear consent notice for the specific purpose of data use (PIB explainer).

Pay-per-lead services that run their own ads and pass on genuine enquiries are different, but ask whether each lead is exclusive to you, and judge them on site visits like any other source.

How should you measure real estate lead generation?

Once a month, put every lead source in one table and compare them the same way:

ColumnWhy it matters
Spend (money and time)Free sources still cost hours
EnquiriesVolume only, not quality
Contacted and qualifiedShows follow-up quality and lead fit
Site visitsThe number that predicts bookings
BookingsThe real result
Cost per site visitThe figure to compare sources on

Move budget towards the sources that produce visits most cheaply and cut the rest, however good their lead counts look.
