Ask builders and brokers in India about leads and you rarely hear “we get too few”. You hear “the leads are junk”, “the numbers don’t pick up”, “they’re attracting people who can’t afford the flat”, or “we got 300 leads and four site visits”. So this guide covers both halves of real estate lead generation: where leads come from, and how to make sure the ones you pay for turn into site visits.

First, define what counts as a lead

Most arguments between builders, CPs and agencies come from counting different things. Agree on these four stages before you spend a rupee:

StageWhat it means
EnquirySomeone left a name and number — a form, call, WhatsApp message or portal lead.
ContactedYour team or chatbot actually spoke to them.
QualifiedBudget, configuration, location and timeline match something you can sell.
Site visitThey came to the site or sample flat. This is the number that predicts bookings.

Judge every lead source on cost per site visit, not cost per enquiry. A source with cheap enquiries and no visits is the most expensive one you have.

Developers usually need a mix: CPs and portals for reach, plus a direct source they own — their own ads and landing page — so they aren’t dependent on anyone else’s leads.

Paid lead generation: Meta and Google ads

Your own ads produce leads nobody else receives, which is why they convert better than shared portal enquiries when handled well. The two platforms do different jobs:

  • Google search catches people typing “3 BHK in Sector 150 Noida” or your project name. Fewer leads, usually closer to buying. See Google Ads for real estate.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) reaches people before they search — essential for launches, EOI drives and retargeting. See Meta ads for real estate in India.

Whichever you use, send ads to a project landing page, not your homepage or a portal listing, and track every lead back to the ad that produced it.

Free ways to get real estate leads

If you are a broker or channel partner starting out, these cost time rather than money:

  1. Set up your Google Business Profile with your service area, real photos and regular posts. Our local SEO guide for agents walks through it.
  2. Ask every buyer and seller for referrals — at booking, at possession, and a few months after.
  3. Post short walkthrough and locality videos on Instagram and YouTube with your WhatsApp number on screen.
  4. Work with a few developers as a registered CP and use their launch material — see channel partner in real estate.
  5. Keep an opt-in WhatsApp list of past enquiries and send useful updates, not daily spam.
  6. Reply fast to every enquiry you already get. The cheapest lead is the one you don’t lose.

Why you get junk leads — and how to fix it

Most “fake leads” are not fake. They are real people who were the wrong audience, clicked too easily, or were called too late. The usual causes and fixes:

ProblemFix
Meta instant form too easy to submitUse the higher-intent form type with a review step, and add 2–3 qualifying questions (budget, configuration, timeline).
Wrong audience — people who can’t afford itShow the price band in the ad and on the form. Buyers outside the budget filter themselves out.
Ads optimising for form fillsSend qualified leads and site visits back to Meta (Conversions API) and Google (offline conversions) so they learn what a buyer looks like.
Irrelevant Google searchesNegative keywords: rent, PG, jobs, resale (for new launches), free, course.
Numbers don’t pick upReply on WhatsApp within seconds; many buyers won’t answer an unknown number but will reply to a message with the brochure.
Called once, then forgottenA follow-up sequence: WhatsApp, call, reminder, update — spread over days, not one attempt.

Speed matters more than volume

Property buyers enquire with several projects in the same evening. The first project to reply with the price sheet and a visit slot usually gets the visit. That is why the fastest improvement for most teams isn’t more ad budget — it’s an instant reply to every enquiry, day and night.

Should you buy real estate leads or databases?

Search for “real estate leads” and you will find sellers of phone number lists and “verified buyer databases”. Be careful:

  • The people on those lists never asked to hear from you, so contact rates are low and complaints are common.
  • Promotional calls and messages to numbers registered on the DND list can breach TRAI’s commercial communication rules.
  • India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 requires a lawful basis, usually consent, for using someone’s personal data.

Pay-per-lead services that run their own ads and pass on genuine enquiries are different — but ask whether the lead is exclusive to you, and judge them on site visits like any other source.

Measure every source the same way

Once a month, put every lead source in one table: spend, enquiries, contacted, qualified, site visits, bookings, and cost per site visit. Move budget towards the sources that produce visits most cheaply and cut the rest, however good their lead counts look.
