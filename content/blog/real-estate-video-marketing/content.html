The Anatomy of a High-Converting 20-Second Reel

To maximize lead generation from short-form video, follow our battle-tested formula:

  1. The Hook (Seconds 0–3): Show the most jaw-dropping feature immediately with a provocative caption: "Can you believe this 3 BHK in Noida is under ₹90 Lakhs?"
  2. The Spatial Reality (Seconds 3–12): Fast-cut movement showing the balcony view, master bedroom dimensions, and modern modular kitchen.
  3. Financial Advantage (Seconds 12–16): Display the starting EMI or tranche launch pricing clearly on screen.
  4. The 1-Tap Call to Action (Seconds 16–20): "Comment AUDIT or tap WhatsApp below to get the official RERA price sheet and brochure instantly."
