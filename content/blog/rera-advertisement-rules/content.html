The days of launching unapproved "soft launches" or teasing residential towers on WhatsApp before state approvals are permanently over in Indian real estate. Over the past 18 months, regulatory bodies across Uttar Pradesh (UP RERA), Delhi (D-RERA), and Haryana (HRERA) have issued hundreds of show-cause notices and levied penalties worth crores of rupees on developers and channel partners for non-compliant marketing.

Every digital banner, newspaper jacket, outdoor billboard, and Instagram Reel is subject to strict regulatory scrutiny. Here is the definitive compliance checklist for developers and marketing agencies running RERA-compliant Meta and Google campaigns in North India.

The Core Legal Framework: Section 3 & Section 11(2)

Under Section 3 of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, no promoter can solicit investments or buyer interest without active registration. Furthermore, Section 11(2) mandates that every advertisement or prospectus issued by the promoter must contain the following prominent disclosures:

  • The Project RERA Registration Number: Issued by the respective state regulatory authority.
  • The Official State RERA Website URL: E.g., https://up-rera.in for Uttar Pradesh or https://haryanarera.gov.in for Gurugram/Faridabad.
  • Scannable QR Code: Directing users straight to the project's public profile on the authority portal, allowing immediate inspection of approvals, sanctioned layouts, and possession deadlines.

The 5-Point Creative Compliance Checklist

Before any creative goes live across your media plan, ensure it passes these five strict regulatory checks:

  1. Exact Legal Project Name: Verify that the project name on your creative matches the exact registered title on the RERA portal. For example, if a project is registered as "Royal Residency Phase II", advertising it simply as "The Royal Heights" without clarification invites immediate regulatory scrutiny.
  2. Promoter Identity: Disclose the actual company or LLP name holding project development rights.
  3. Verifiable Amenities: Never display 3D visual renders of luxury amenities (e.g., helipads, artificial lakes, Olympic sports complexes) unless they are formally sanctioned in the approved building layout plans submitted to the development authority.
  4. Transparent Pricing: Base Selling Price (BSP) advertisements must explicitly state whether statutory taxes (GST), development charges (EDC/IDC), and preferred location charges (PLC) are included or extra.
  5. RERA Authority Disclaimer: Include standard text advising buyers to review verified project details directly on the state regulator's portal prior to signing any booking agreements.

Channel Partner & Agency Rules (Section 10)

Channel partners frequently make the fatal mistake of believing RERA rules only apply to builders. Under Section 10 of the Act:

"Every real estate agent registered under section 9 shall quote their registration number in every sale facilitated by them and in all advertisements, brochures, and marketing literature."

When an agency or broker runs campaigns for a builder project, their marketing must display both credentials:

  • The Project RERA: UPRERAAGT12345 / Project UPRERAPRJ67890
  • The Channel Partner RERA: Agent RERA Registration: UPRERAAGT99999

By enforcing complete regulatory transparency on your custom project landing pages, builders protect their legal standing, prevent costly administrative freezes, and build profound trust with discerning property buyers.
