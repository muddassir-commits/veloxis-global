A real estate referral program is a simple, written promise: when someone sends you a buyer who books, you thank them with a reward agreed in advance. For developers and brokers in India, the best referrers are happy buyers, residents of past projects, channel partners and local professionals.

A good program makes referring easy, pays rewards on time, and stays within RERA, tax and data protection rules.

This guide explains why referrals work, who and when to ask, reward ideas and payout rules, the RERA, tax and consent points to check, agent-to-agent referrals, and how to track referrals so nobody is forgotten.

Why do referrals work in real estate?

Buying a home is one of the biggest decisions a family makes, and buyers look for someone they can trust. A recommendation from a friend or relative who already bought from you carries that trust. In Nielsen's Global Trust in Advertising survey (2015), 83% of online respondents said they completely or somewhat trusted recommendations from people they know, more than any form of advertising.

Referred buyers also arrive with context. They have heard what the project is like, how the builder or broker behaves, and roughly what it costs. That usually means fewer basic questions and more serious conversations. Referrals don't replace ads and follow-up, but they add buyers you didn't pay to reach. Who can refer buyers to you?

  • Past buyers and residents. The most trusted referrers, especially after possession, when they live in the home and neighbours visit.
  • Current buyers in under-construction projects. They often know others looking in the same area, and a good construction update gives them something to share.
  • Channel partners and brokers. Professionals who bring buyers for a commission under a written agreement. Our guide to channel partners in real estate explains how those agreements work.
  • Local professionals. Chartered accountants, interior designers, architects and others who meet people planning to buy. Be careful here: if they refer regularly for money, see the RERA section below.
  • Your own team and their networks. Staff outside sales can refer friends and family under clear rules.

When should you ask for a referral?

Ask when the buyer is happiest, not when you need sales. Good moments include:

  • Right after booking, when the excitement is fresh and they are telling family and friends.
  • When the home loan is approved or the agreement for sale is registered: a big relief.
  • At key handover, the most emotional moment in the whole process.
  • After you solve a problem well. A complaint handled quickly often creates a loyal supporter.

Keep the ask simple and low-pressure. A short WhatsApp message works well:

Congratulations on your new home, {{name}}. If any friend or relative is also looking, I'd be glad to help them the same way. You can share my number: {{number}}.

What rewards work for a real estate referral program?

There is no single right reward; the best one is something the referrer values and you can deliver on time. Common choices:

RewardBest forWatch out for
Credit on remaining instalments or maintenanceBuyers still paying for an under-construction homeRecord it in the buyer's account in writing
Gift (appliances, furniture vouchers, interior upgrade)Buyers near possession or recently moved inDeliver it on the promised date
Benefit for both sides (a waived charge for the new buyer too)Making the referral feel like a favour, not a saleMust not conflict with the price list or agreement
Commission under a written agreementChannel partners, brokers and regular professional referrersRERA agent registration, TDS and GST (see below)
Recognition (a thank-you event, a mention in the resident newsletter with permission)Residents of delivered projectsGet consent before naming anyone

Whatever you choose, write down:

  1. What counts as a referral: for example, a buyer who was not already in your records and who names the referrer before or at the first site visit.
  2. When the reward is earned: usually once the referred buyer's agreement for sale is registered, or after a set number of payments, so you don't pay for bookings that are cancelled.
  3. When it is paid: a fixed number of days after it is earned.
  4. What happens if two people claim the same buyer: usually the first one recorded in your system.

Pay on time, every time. A late or disputed reward turns a supporter into a critic, and they will tell people that too.

What RERA, tax and consent rules apply to referrals?

RERA agent registration. Section 2(zm) of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 defines a real estate agent as someone who negotiates or acts for another person in a sale and receives remuneration or commission for it, and Section 9 requires agents to register with the state RERA before facilitating sales in registered projects. A buyer who once recommends a friend and receives a thank-you gift is very different from someone who regularly brings buyers for a fee. If a person refers buyers as a regular business, work with them as a RERA-registered agent or channel partner. Check the position with your state RERA or lawyer.

TDS on commission. Commission and brokerage payments are subject to TDS under India's income tax law, above a yearly threshold. The rate and threshold have changed in recent years, and section numbers can change with new legislation, so check the current position on the Income Tax Department's portal or with your chartered accountant before paying.

GST. Brokerage and commission services generally attract GST when the person providing them is GST-registered (see CBIC GST). Pay against a proper invoice.

Gifts and discounts. Rewards to individuals can have their own tax treatment. Ask your CA how to record discounts, credits and gifts before you launch the program.

Consent for contact details. When a buyer shares a friend's number, contact the friend once, say who referred them, and stop if they aren't interested. Keep referral data only for this purpose, in line with India's data protection framework. Asking the referrer to pass your number to their friend, rather than the other way round, avoids the problem entirely.

How do agent-to-agent referrals work?

Agent-to-agent referrals work when one broker passes a client to another who covers a different city, locality or property type, in return for a share of the commission agreed in writing. A broker in Kanpur whose client is moving to Noida, for example, can refer them to a trusted Noida broker. Keep it clean: both agents RERA-registered where required, the split and payment timing written down before the client is introduced, and the client told who will handle them. For developers, the same idea is formalised as a channel partner program.

How do you track real estate referrals?

Most referral programs fail because nobody records who referred whom. Keep it simple:

  • Add a "referred by" field to every lead in your CRM or sheet, and fill it in on the first call.
  • Give referrers an easy way to share: a short WhatsApp message with your number, a referral card, or a link to your project page with a code they can quote.
  • Ask every new lead "How did you hear about us?" Many referred buyers don't mention it unless asked.
  • Tell the referrer what happened. A message like "Your friend visited today, thank you!" keeps them involved, even before any reward is due.
  • Review monthly: referrals received, site visits, bookings and rewards paid.

What are common referral program mistakes?

  • Unclear terms, so referrers and the company remember the deal differently.
  • Late rewards, which hurt trust more than having no program at all.
  • Asking unhappy buyers. Fix the problem first; ask later.
  • Paying regular referrers commission without checking RERA rules.
  • Over-promising. Don't let referrers promise returns, discounts or dates you haven't agreed to. They speak for your brand.
  • Forgetting the referred buyer. They expect the same quality of service their friend received. Deliver it.

How to start a real estate referral program this month

  1. Write a one-page referral policy: who can refer, the reward, when it is earned and paid, and how disputes are settled.
  2. Add a "referred by" field to your lead records.
  3. Make a list of buyers who booked or got possession in the last year and were happy.
  4. Send each one a personal thank-you message with the referral offer (our real estate email marketing guide covers the email version).
  5. Add the referral ask to your booking and handover process, so it happens every time.
