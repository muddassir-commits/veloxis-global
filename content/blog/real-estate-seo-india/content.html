Real estate SEO in India means building pages that rank for searches you can actually win: your project name, your builder or agency name, and locality plus configuration searches such as "3 BHK in Sector 150 Noida". Portals own the broad searches, so focus on project and locality pages, a complete Google Business Profile, fast mobile pages and genuine reviews.

This guide is for builders, brokers, property dealers and channel partners (CPs). It covers what real estate SEO is, the four types of SEO, the keywords worth targeting, project and locality pages, SEO for brokers, local SEO for real estate agents, how AI search changes things, how to judge real estate SEO services, and how to measure results. Every recommendation links to Google's own documentation where one exists.

What is real estate SEO?

Real estate SEO (search engine optimisation) is setting up your website and online profiles so Google shows them when buyers search for the projects, localities and services you sell. Done well, it brings enquiries that cost nothing per click and keep coming while the page ranks.

It differs from SEO for a shop or software company in three ways:

  • Portals dominate broad searches. For "2 BHK in Lucknow", the first page is usually property portals and aggregators. Competing head-on rarely pays.
  • Searches are hyper-local. Buyers search by sector, corridor and project name. Each locality is its own small market.
  • Projects have a life cycle. Searches for a project rise around launch and possession and fade after sell-out. Your pages need to follow that cycle.

So real estate SEO is less about one big keyword and more about owning many small, high-intent searches that portals don't serve well.

What are the four types of SEO in real estate?

The four types are on-page, technical, local and off-page SEO. All four matter for a property business, but on-page and local usually bring the fastest results.

Which real estate keywords should you target?

Target keywords by how close the searcher is to booking a site visit, not by search volume. Work through them in this order:

  1. Project and builder names. Someone searching your project's name is already interested. If your own page doesn't rank first, a portal or another broker takes that buyer. Fix this first.
  2. Locality plus configuration. "3 BHK in Gomti Nagar Extension", "plots on Sultanpur Road", "ready to move flats in Sector 150". Lower volume, high intent, often winnable.
  3. Agent and service searches. "Property dealer in Kalyanpur", "real estate agent near me", "commercial property broker in Noida". These are mostly won on Google Maps through local SEO.
  4. Questions buyers ask before buying. "What is EOI in real estate?", "How to check a RERA number?" Guides that answer these build trust early. See our guides on EOI and the RERA number check for examples.

Avoid chasing "property in Noida" on a new site. Spend the same effort on ten locality and project pages instead. Search Console (below) shows which of these searches you already appear for.

How do you build project and locality pages that rank?

Give every project and every locality its own page, with the key facts written as text and an enquiry option on the first screen. The page that ranks for a project search should also turn the visitor into an enquiry, which is why SEO and the real estate landing page belong together.

  • Project name, locality and configuration in the title and main heading, for example "Project Name: 2 & 3 BHK Apartments on Shaheed Path, Lucknow". Google recommends unique, descriptive titles for each page (Google).
  • Real text, not only images. Price band, sizes, amenities, possession date and the RERA number should be on the page, not hidden in a render or PDF. RERA rules also require the registration number in advertisements; see RERA advertisement rules.
  • A short FAQ with the questions your sales team hears daily: parking, loans, possession, maintenance.
  • Location detail: honest travel times to the metro, schools, hospitals and highways, plus a map.
  • Fast mobile load with a clear enquiry button and WhatsApp option.

When a project sells out, don't delete its page. Keep it with a "sold out" note and links to newer projects, or use a permanent (301) redirect to the closest live page, which Google treats as a strong signal that the new URL replaces the old one (Google). How does SEO for brokers and property dealers work?

SEO for brokers is mostly local SEO plus a small, original website: a verified Google Business Profile, one page per locality you work in, your own pages for the projects you sell, and steady genuine reviews. Brokers rarely beat portals or developers for project names, but they can win "property dealer in [area]" and locality searches.

What is local SEO for real estate agents?

Local SEO for real estate agents is getting your Google Business Profile and website to show in map and "near me" results for the areas you serve. Google says local results are based on relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to pay for a better local ranking (Google Business Profile Help).

Steps for an agent or small developer:

  1. Create and verify your Business Profile. Verification makes the profile more likely to show in search, according to Google.
  2. Choose the primary category that fits best (for example "Real estate agency" or "Real estate agent") and add secondary ones only if true.
  3. Set service areas if you don't receive visitors at an office; Google's service-area guidance says to remove the address from your profile if you don't serve customers there (Google).
  4. Fill every field: description, services, photos of your office and real sites, opening hours if you have an office.
  5. Ask every satisfied buyer or seller for a review and reply to all reviews. Never buy or swap them; Google's policies forbid fake engagement (Google).
  6. Keep the same name, phone and website on your site, profile and every directory.
  7. Post updates on new listings, site-visit days and locality news.

Is SEO being replaced by AI search?

No, but it is changing. Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode use Google's normal search index, and Google says no special markup is needed to appear in them beyond being indexed and eligible for a snippet (Google). ChatGPT, Perplexity and Copilot also build answers from web pages and cite them.

That rewards what good real estate SEO already needed: real facts in plain text, honest answers, FAQs, and consistent business details across the web. Searches with intent to buy, such as a project name or a locality and budget, still send people to websites. Step-by-step advice is in our guide to generative engine optimization (GEO).

How do you choose real estate SEO services in India?

Choose a provider that works on the searches that bring site visits (project, locality and agent searches), explains its work in plain language, and reports from Search Console and your lead sheet rather than vanity numbers. Questions to ask before you hire anyone:

  • Which project and locality pages will you build or fix first, and why?
  • Will you set up Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and the Business Profile in my name?
  • How will you get links? (Anyone selling bulk links is risking a penalty under Google's link spam policy.)
  • How will you show enquiries and site visits from organic search, not just traffic?

Be wary of guaranteed rankings. Google's own guidance on hiring an SEO says no one can guarantee a number one ranking.

How long does real estate SEO take, and how do you measure it?

Expect weeks for a new page to be indexed and months for positions to settle; a new website takes longer. Most builders run ads for a launch and build SEO alongside, so organic enquiries take a growing share over time. Measure with free tools:

  • Google Search Console shows the searches you appear for, position and clicks (Performance report). Watch project-name and locality searches first.
  • Business Profile performance shows calls, website clicks and direction requests from Maps.
  • Your lead sheet or CRM should record "Google search" and "Google Maps" as sources, so you count site visits and bookings, not just traffic.

What are the most common real estate SEO mistakes?

  • One "Projects" page for everything; it can't rank for five project names.
  • Brochure details only in PDFs or images.
  • Deleting sold-out project pages instead of keeping or redirecting them.
  • Copying the developer's text onto a CP or broker site.
  • Buying cheap backlinks or reviews.
  • Ignoring the Google Business Profile, often the fastest local win.
  • Expecting results in a month. Plan SEO alongside ads, not instead of them.
