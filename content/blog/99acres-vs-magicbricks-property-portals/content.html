What differs is which one brings more genuine enquiries in your city, locality and budget, so test one for a month and compare cost per site visit.

How do property portals make money?

Portals are free for buyers and earn from advertisers: brokers, builders and owners who pay for listings and visibility.

  • Broker and dealer packages: a number of listings for a period, with higher placement or "featured" tags in bigger packages.
  • Builder and project promotion: project pages, banners, home-page and locality slots, email and app pushes.
  • Owner plans: a free basic listing, with paid upgrades for visibility or help from the portal's team.
  • Other services: home loans, rental agreements, movers and data reports.

A portal earns from the number of advertisers it sells to, not from your bookings. That is how classified marketplaces work, and it explains most broker complaints.

Does 99acres charge money? Is MagicBricks free?

Both are free for buyers and tenants, free for a basic owner listing, and paid for brokers, dealers and builders who want reach. Free listings have limits on number and placement, and often sit below featured and premium listings in busy localities, so they get few views. That is why many brokers who post free get few calls, buy a package, and then find the bigger package is what moves them up the page.

99acres agent package price and MagicBricks plans for agents are not one fixed rate: they depend on city, number of listings, duration and visibility, and are often negotiated. Before you pay, get a written quote and check how long the package runs, whether unused listings carry over, and whether the price includes GST. Ask each salesperson three questions:

  1. How many enquiries did similar listings in my locality and budget get last quarter?
  2. How many other advertisers are paying for the same locality and budget?
  3. Can I start with the smallest package for one month?

If they can't answer the first two with numbers, be careful with any "guaranteed leads" promise.

Are 99acres and MagicBricks reliable for buyers?

As platforms, yes; individual listings still need checking. Stale and duplicate listings are common, and some "owner" listings are posted by brokers. The same flat can appear on several portals at different prices because different brokers list it, so no portal's price is final: compare similar listings in the same society and check recent registered sale values where your state publishes them.

  1. Use the verified filters.
  2. Confirm any new project's RERA number on the state RERA website (our RERA number check guide shows how).
  3. Visit before paying any token amount.
  4. Be wary of prices far below the rest of the locality.
  5. Pay only to the owner or developer's account, never cash to a stranger.

On coverage: portals list both resale and new projects, but new projects are also marketed heavily through developers' own pages and channel partners, so check the developer's site and RERA filing too.

Are portal leads good for sellers?

They are real people, but rarely exclusive. A portal enquiry is someone who clicked your listing, but:

  • Buyers usually enquire on several similar listings, so other advertisers call them too.
  • Many enquiries are early browsers, people checking rents, or other brokers.
  • Numbers called hours later are often not answered.

So lead quality depends heavily on what you do in the first minutes after an enquiry arrives.

How do you get more site visits from portal leads?

Reply first and clearly, qualify lightly, and offer specific visit slots.

  1. Reply within minutes, on WhatsApp and by call. Send the project name, price band, location pin and one photo.
  2. Ask two questions: budget and timeline. See our lead qualification questions.
  3. Offer two site-visit slots, such as "Saturday 11 AM or Sunday 4 PM?".
  4. Follow up three times over a week, each time with something new: floor plan, walkthrough video, payment plan.
  5. Log every lead with its source in a CRM, so you can compare cost per site visit by portal; see real estate CRM in India.

Steps 1 and 4 are what WhatsApp automation handles well: an instant reply at 11 PM on a Sunday.

Which should you choose?

  • Owner selling or renting one property: post free on 99acres and MagicBricks (and NoBroker to avoid brokers), with good photos and an honest price. Pay only if you get no calls after a couple of weeks.
  • Broker or CP: test the smallest package on the portal strongest in your localities for a month, track cost per site visit, then decide. Show your RERA agent registration where required.
  • Builder with a new project: keep a portal project page for presence, but put most budget into your own project landing page, Meta and Google ads and an instant WhatsApp reply, as in the Real Estate Lead System.

What are common mistakes with property portals?

  • Buying a long package before testing one month.
  • Calling leads the next morning.
  • Posting the same listing many times.
  • Missing price, carpet area or RERA number, or photos that don't match.
  • Not tracking the source of each lead.
  • Relying on one channel only.
