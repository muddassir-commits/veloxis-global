## 1. Editorial Rules for the Daily 2 Blog Machine
Every blog published in this system must follow the **proven Veloxis formula**:
1. **Word Count**: 1,500–2,000 words in `content.html`.
2. **Imagery**: Exactly 6 images per post (1 Cover at 1600×1000 + 5 inline 1600×1000 photos placed immediately before different `<h2>` headings, complete with descriptive alt text and captions).
3. **Internal Linking**:
   - 1 link to its dedicated service page (`/services/paid-ads`, `/services/high-converting-landing-pages`, `/services/ai-automation`, or `/channel-partners`).
   - 1 link to its cluster pillar.
   - 2–4 live sibling posts.
   - Closing paragraph link to `/free-audit`.
4. **FAQ Schema**: 7–9 direct answer questions backed by `FAQPage` JSON-LD schema (40–50 word snippet formulation).
5. **Wikipedia / Entity Links**: `about` schema referencing recognized entities (RERA, Info Edge, MagicBricks, Meta, Google).
6. **Zero Fluff**: Grounded in real Indian real estate realities (CPL vs CPSV, RERA sections, circle rates, 70/30 commission splits).
